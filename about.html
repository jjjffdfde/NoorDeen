About Our Mission

Dedicated to serving seekers of truth worldwide with verified Quranic and Sunnah knowledge.

1. Purpose & Vision

The Islamic Knowledge Portal was established to provide an authentic, universally accessible, and dignified digital repository of core Islamic texts and teachings. In an era saturated with unverified information, sectarian polemics, and inaccurate translations, our mission is to offer pure, verified knowledge derived from classical Islamic scholarship and mainstream consensus.

Our platform is architected to serve both lifelong Muslims seeking daily spiritual tools and seekers of truth exploring the beauty, peace, and rational coherence of Islam.

2. Strict Content Integrity Policy

  • No AI-Generated Religious Texts: Quranic Arabic text is strictly preserved according to the authentic Uthmanic Mushaf. We never generate, alter, or synthesize Quranic Arabic or Hadith texts using artificial intelligence.
  • Scholarly Attribution: Hadiths are accompanied by verifiable collection references, book numbers, hadith numbers, and established authenticity gradings (Sahih / Hasan).
  • Multilingual Accuracy: Translations are provided only where authorized, verified scholar-led translations exist, with explicit attribution to the translator.

3. Privacy & Non-Commercial Dignity

We believe that spiritual reading and contemplation should be free from invasive tracking, aggressive marketing, and intrusive popups. Bookmarks, recitation counts, and reading positions are maintained locally in your browser and are never transmitted to third parties without your explicit consent.

Religious Legal Disclaimer

The educational materials, prayer guidelines, and jurisprudence articles published on this portal reflect classical scholarly consensus and are intended solely for general instructional purposes. They do not substitute for personalized legal rulings (Fatwas) rendered by certified local Islamic scholars and Muftis who can assess specific personal circumstances.
