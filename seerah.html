The Prophetic Biography (As-Seerah)

The noble life, exemplary character, and eternal mercy of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum).

ERA 1: Pre-Islamic & Early Life (570 CE – 595 CE) النسب الشريف والمولد المبارك

Blessed Lineage, Birth, and Childhood

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born on Monday, 12th of Rabi al-Awwal in the Year of the Elephant (570 CE), in the noble Hashimite clan of Quraysh in Makkah. His father Abdullah passed away before his birth. He was nursed by Halimah as-Sa'diyyah in the desert, where he acquired pure speech and robust health. Even in his youth, he was renowned among all tribes as Al-Sadiq (The Truthful) and Al-Amin (The Trustworthy).

Key Milestones: Year of the Elephant • Care by Halimah, Aminah & Abdul Muttalib • Hilf al-Fudul (Pact of the Virtuous) for social justice.
ERA 2: Makkan Period (610 CE) بدء الوحي في غار حراء

The First Revelation in Cave Hira

As he approached forty, the Prophet ﷺ loved contemplation in Cave Hira. In Ramadan of 610 CE, Archangel Jibreel appeared and commanded: "Iqra!" (Read!). Jibreel recited Surah Al-Alaq (96:1-5). Returning with trembling awe, his noble wife Khadijah (RA) comforted him with immortal words: "Never! By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you; you keep ties of kinship, help the poor, support the destitute, and stand by truthful causes."

Key Milestones: Descent of Surah Al-Alaq 1-5 • Reassurance by Sayyidah Khadijah • Confirmation by Waraqah ibn Nawfal.
ERA 3: Makkan Period (619 CE – 621 CE) عام الحزن والإسراء والمعراج

The Year of Sorrow & Al-Isra wal-Mi'raj

Following the passing of Khadijah (RA) and Abu Talib, and severe persecution at Ta'if, Allah honored His Messenger with the miraculous Night Journey (Al-Isra) from Masjid al-Haram to Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem, and the Heavenly Ascension (Al-Mi'raj) where the gift of the five daily prayers was established.

Key Milestones: Supplication of mercy for Ta'if • Al-Isra to Jerusalem • Ascension through seven heavens • Gift of 5 Daily Prayers.
ERA 4: Madinan Period (622 CE) الهجرة النبوية المباركة

The Great Migration (Hijrah) to Madinah

Accompanied by Abu Bakr (RA), the Prophet ﷺ migrated safely to Madinah after three nights in Cave Thawr. In Madinah, he constructed Masjid an-Nabawi, instituted brotherhood between Muhajirun and Ansar, and authored the Constitution of Madinah—the world's first written constitution guaranteeing equal civic rights and religious freedom.

Key Milestones: Cave Thawr preservation • Building Masjid an-Nabawi • Constitution of Madinah.
ERA 5: Farewell & Legacy (630 CE – 632 CE) فتح مكة وحجة الوداع

The Conquest of Makkah & Farewell Sermon

In 630 CE, the Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah in peaceful triumph, destroying idols and declaring universal amnesty: "Go, for you are free." During the Farewell Pilgrimage at Arafat, he delivered the historic Universal Human Rights Sermon, proclaiming the sacredness of human life, racial equality, and women's rights.

Key Milestones: Bloodless conquest of Makkah • Farewell Sermon at Mount Arafat • Perfection of the religion (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:3).
