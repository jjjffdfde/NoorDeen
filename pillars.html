The Five Pillars of Islam (أركان الإسلام)

"Islam is built upon five: testifying that none is worthy of worship except Allah and that Muhammad is His Messenger, establishing prayer, giving zakah, pilgrimage to the House, and fasting Ramadan." (Sahih al-Bukhari #8)

PILLAR 1 الشهادة

Shahadah — The Declaration of Faith

The fundamental proclamation of monotheism: "Ash-hadu al-laa ilaaha illal-laahu wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhoo wa Rasooluh" (I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and Muhammad is His servant and Messenger). It requires inward certainty, sincere submission, and outward rejection of all polytheism and false deities.

Proof: Surah Muhammad (47:19): "So know that there is no deity except Allah..."
PILLAR 2 الصلاة

Salah — The Five Daily Prayers

The mandatory five prayers (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) performed facing the Ka'bah. Prayer serves as the direct link connecting the servant to the Creator throughout the cycle of day and night, washing away sins and instilling tranquility.

Proof: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:43): "And establish prayer and give zakah and bow with those who bow."
PILLAR 3 الزكاة

Zakat — The Obligatory Almsgiving

The annual 2.5% contribution on qualifying surplus wealth held for one full lunar year above the Nisab threshold. It purifies wealth, prevents wealth hoarding, fosters communal compassion, and guarantees vital financial assistance to the poor, orphans, and destitute.

Proof: Surah At-Tawbah (9:60): "Zakah expenditures are only for the poor and for the needy..."
PILLAR 4 الصوم

Sawm — Fasting the Month of Ramadan

Abstaining from all food, drink, and intimate relations from dawn (Fajr) until sunset (Maghrib) for the entire lunar month of Ramadan. It builds self-control, spiritual empathy for the hungry, and heightens Taqwa (God-consciousness).

Proof: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:183): "O you who have believed, decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you..."
PILLAR 5 الحج

Hajj — The Pilgrimage to Makkah

The pilgrimage to the Sacred House of Allah in Makkah, obligatory once in a lifetime for every Muslim who possesses physical health and financial capability. Millions gather as equals in simple white unstitched garments (Ihram), stripping away all worldly divisions.

Proof: Surah Ali 'Imran (3:97): "And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House - for whoever is able to find thereto a way."
