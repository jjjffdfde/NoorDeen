Stories of the Prophets (عليهم السلام)

"There was certainly in their stories a lesson for those of understanding." (Surah Yusuf 12:111)

Adam (عليه السلام)

Father of Mankind • Mentioned 25 Times in Quran
آدم عليه السلام

Created by Allah from clay and granted the knowledge of all things. When tempted by Iblis regarding the tree, Adam and Hawwa (Eve) turned in sincere repentance, teaching humanity the eternal door of Tawbah: "Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers" (7:23).

Key Lessons: Supreme honor of divine knowledge • The poison of pride (Kibr) • Repentance washes away sin.

Nuh / Noah (عليه السلام)

Shaikh al-Anbiya • Preached Tawhid for 950 Years
نوح عليه السلام

Preached pure monotheism with immense patience day and night. When the ruling oligarchs persisted in idol worship, he built the Ark under divine inspiration. The believers were delivered from the global deluge while arrogance was wiped clean.

Key Lessons: Perseverance without relying on immediate numbers • Spiritual kinship transcends blood.

Ibrahim / Abraham (عليه السلام)

Khalilullah (Intimate Friend of Allah) • Builder of the Ka'bah
إبراهيم عليه السلام

The patriarch of pure monotheism. Thrown into Nimrod's blazing fire, Allah commanded it to be coolness and peace. Tested with leaving baby Ismail and Hajar in barren Makkah and submitting to sacrifice, he surrendered completely, earning the title of an Ummah in himself (Surah An-Nahl 16:120).

Key Lessons: Complete trust (Tawakkul) • Rational debate over superstitions • Raising the foundations of the Holy Ka'bah.

Musa / Moses (عليه السلام)

Kalimullah (Spoken to by Allah) • Liberator of Bani Isra'il
موسى عليه السلام

Confronted Pharaoh's brutal tyranny with the staff and divine signs. When trapped against the Red Sea, he proclaimed: "No! Indeed, with me is my Lord; He will guide me!" (26:62). Allah parted the sea and revealed the Holy Torah.

Key Lessons: Speaking truth to power • Liberating the oppressed • Steadfast faith in divine deliverance.

Isa / Jesus (عليه السلام)

Ruhullah & Kalimatullah • Messenger of Peace and Mercy
عيسى عليه السلام

Born miraculously of the virgin Maryam (Mary). He healed the blind, cured lepers, and raised the dead by Allah's permission. Sent with the Injil to confirm the Torah and announce the coming of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Ahmad). Allah raised him unto Himself and he was neither killed nor crucified (Surah An-Nisa 4:157).

Key Lessons: Spiritual humility over hypocritical legalism • Clear affirmation of monotheism (servant of Allah, not divine).
