Step-by-Step Android Installation Guide (Sideloading)

1

Download the APK File

Tap the "Download Release APK" button above. When your mobile browser displays the default Android warning ("File might be harmful... Do you want to download islamic-portal-release.apk anyway?"), tap "Download anyway". This standard warning appears for every application downloaded outside the Google Play Store.

2

Open the Downloaded File

Once the download completes, tap the download notification in your notification tray or open your device's Files or Downloads app and tap islamic-portal-release.apk.

3

Allow Installation from Browser (One-Time Prompt)

If prompted with: "For your security, your phone is currently not allowed to install unknown apps from this source":

  • Tap Settings on the pop-up dialog.
  • Toggle on the switch for "Allow from this source" for Chrome, Edge, or Samsung Internet.
  • Tap the Back button to return to the installer.
4

Complete Installation

Tap "Install". Google Play Protect will perform an automated safety scan in the background. Once installed, tap "Open" to launch your new offline Islamic library!
