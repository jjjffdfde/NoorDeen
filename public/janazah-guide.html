Complete Step-by-Step Janazah Guide

"Whoever follows a funeral procession until prayer is offered will receive one Qirat of reward; and whoever follows it until burial will receive two Qirats, each like Mount Uhud." (Sahih al-Bukhari #1325). Master the 4 Takbirs, adult and child supplications, comparative Madhhab analysis, and cemetery etiquette.

1. Theological Foundation and Communal Obligation (Fard Kifayah)

Salat al-Janazah (the Islamic funeral prayer) is a distinguished, unique collective ritual of deep spiritual solemnity. Unlike the five daily obligatory prayers, it consists entirely of standing (Qiyam), with no bowing (Ruku') and no prostration (Sujud). It is not offered to worship the deceased, but rather to petition the Almighty Lord as an interceding community for mercy, forgiveness, and steadfastness for a departed brother or sister in faith.

By consensus of Muslim scholarship, performing Janazah is classified as Fard Kifayah (a communal obligation). If a portion of the Muslim community fulfills it, the obligation is lifted from the rest of the community. However, if a Muslim passes away in an area and no one gathers to pray over them despite possessing knowledge of the death, the entire locality shares the spiritual culpability. The Prophet ﷺ declared: "A Muslim has five rights over another Muslim: responding to his greeting, visiting the sick, following funeral processions, accepting his invitation, and blessing him when he sneezes." (Sahih al-Bukhari #1240, Sahih Muslim #2162).

2. Standing Posture & Spatial Alignment for Male and Female

Before initiating the prayer, the bier (Janazah) bearing the shrouded deceased is positioned horizontally between the Imam and the Qiblah. The congregation forms odd-numbered rows behind the Imam (a practice recommended by hadith, Sunan Abi Dawud #3166). The spatial alignment of the Imam differs according to the biological sex of the deceased:

For a Deceased Male (Mayyit)

The Imam stands directly aligned with the head or upper chest of the deceased man. This reflects the documented practice of the Prophet ﷺ and his Sahaba during funeral prayers in Madinah (Sunan Abi Dawud #3194).

For a Deceased Female (Mayyitah)

The Imam stands directly aligned with the middle or waist of the deceased woman. This Sunnah was specifically demonstrated by Anas ibn Malik (RA) when asked about the Prophet's ﷺ methodology (Sahih al-Bukhari #1331, Sahih Muslim #964).

3. The Four Takbirs of Salat al-Janazah Step-by-Step

Salat al-Janazah begins without any Adhan or Iqamah. The entire prayer takes place in the standing posture across four pronounced Takbirs:

TAKBIR 1 OF 4 التَّكْبِيرَةُ الْأُولَىٰ — الثَّنَاءُ وَالْفَاتِحَةُ

Opening Takbir: Thana & Surah Al-Fatihah

The Imam raises both hands and says "Allahu Akbar" loudly. The congregation raises their hands and repeats silently, folding their hands across the chest or below the navel. Then recite:

Thana with Funeral Addition (سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ):
سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَىٰ جَدُّكَ، وَجَلَّ ثَنَاؤُكَ، وَلَا إِلَٰهَ غَيْرُكَ.

Subḥānakallāhumma wa bi-ḥamdika, wa tabārakas-muka, wa ta‘ālā jadduka, wa jalla thanā’uka, wa lā ilāha ghayruk.

English Meaning: Glory be to You, O Allah, and all praise is Yours. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, magnified is Your praise, and there is no deity worthy of worship besides You.

پاک ہے تیری ذات اے اللہ اور تیرے ہی لیے تمام تعریفیں ہیں، اور با برکت ہے تیرا نام اور بلند و بالا ہے تیری شان، اور عالی ہے تیری ثناء، اور تیرے سوا کوئی معبود نہیں۔

Surah Al-Fatihah (Obligatory Pillar in Shafi'i & Hanbali):
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ۝ اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ.

Scholarly Note: In the Shafi'i and Hanbali schools, reciting Al-Fatihah after the first Takbir is an obligatory pillar based on the narration of Ibn Abbas in Sahih al-Bukhari #1335. In the Hanafi school, Thana is standard, while Al-Fatihah is recited with the intention of dua.

TAKBIR 2 OF 4 التَّكْبِيرَةُ الثَّانِيَةُ — الصَّلَاةُ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ ﷺ

Second Takbir: Durood Ibrahim upon the Prophet ﷺ

The Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Remain standing upright without bowing. Recite Durood Ibrahim:

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ. اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ.

Allāhumma ṣalli ‘alā Muḥammadin wa ‘alā āli Muḥammad, kamā ṣallayta ‘alā Ibrāheema wa ‘alā āli Ibrāheem, innaka Ḥameedum Majeed...

English Meaning: O Allah, send peace and blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent peace and blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim; indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Full of Glory.

اے اللہ! رحمت نازل فرما حضرت محمد ﷺ پر اور ان کی آل پر جس طرح تو نے رحمت فرمائی حضرت ابراہیم علیہ السلام پر اور ان کی آل پر، بے شک تو قابلِ تعریف اور بڑی بزرگی والا ہے۔

TAKBIR 3 OF 4 التَّكْبِيرَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ — الدُّعَاءُ الْمَأْثُورُ

Third Takbir: Sincere Supplication for the Deceased

The Imam says "Allahu Akbar". This is the heart and primary purpose of Salat al-Janazah. Recite the authentic prophetic supplication designated for an adult or for a child (detailed in Section 4 below).

TAKBIR 4 OF 4 التَّكْبِيرَةُ الرَّابِعَةُ — التَّسْلِيمُ

Fourth Takbir: Final Dua & Tasleem

The Imam pronounces the 4th "Allahu Akbar". A brief pause for general prayer follows (such as "Rabbanā ātinā fid-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fil-ākhirati ḥasanah wa qinā ‘adhāban-nār"). Then conclude with the Tasleem:

  • Hanafi School (Two Tasleems): Turn head right saying "As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatullāh", then left repeating the same.
  • Shafi'i, Hanbali & Maliki Schools (One Tasleem): Turn head slightly right and recite once "As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatullāh".
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ

As-salāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatullāh

4. Authentic Prophetic Supplications (Adult & Child)

The following duas are transmitted through canonical Hadith collections with verified, sound chains of transmission:

1. Authentic Dua for Adult Deceased (Male or Female)

SAHIH
Citations: Sunan Abi Dawud #3201, Jami` at-Tirmidhi #1024, Sunan Ibn Majah #1498
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِحَيِّنَا وَمَيِّتِنَا، وَشَاهِدِنَا وَغَائِبِنَا، وَصَغِيرِنَا وَكَبِيرِنَا، وَذَكَرِنَا وَأُنْثَانَا. اللَّهُمَّ مَنْ أَحْيَيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَأَحْيِهِ عَلَى الْإِسْلَامِ، وَمَنْ تَوَفَّيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَتَوَفَّهُ عَلَى الْإِيمَانِ. اللَّهُمَّ لَا تَحْرِمْنَا أَجْرَهُ، وَلَا تُضِلَّنَا بَعْدَهُ.

Allāhummagh-fir li-ḥayyinaa wa mayyitinā, wa shāhidinā wa ghā'ibinā, wa ṣagheerinā wa kabeerinā, wa dhakarinā wa unthānā. Allāhumma man aḥyaytahū minnā fa-aḥyihī ‘alal-Islām, wa man tawaffaytahū minnā fa-tawaffahū ‘alal-Īmān. Allāhumma lā taḥrimnā ajrahū, wa lā tuḍillanā ba‘dah.

English Translation: O Allah, forgive our living and our deceased, those present among us and those absent, our young and our old, and our males and our females. O Allah, whomever You keep alive among us, cause him to live upon Islam, and whomever You take in death, cause him to die upon Faith (Iman). O Allah, do not deprive us of his reward, and do not cause us to go astray after him.

اے اللہ! بخش دے ہمارے زندوں کو اور ہمارے مُردوں کو، اور ہمارے حاضرین کو اور ہمارے غائبین کو، اور ہمارے چھوٹوں کو اور ہمارے بڑوں کو، اور ہمارے مَردوں کو اور ہماری عورتوں کو۔ اے اللہ! ہم میں سے جسے تو زندہ رکھے اسے اسلام پر زندہ رکھ، اور جسے ہم میں سے موت دے اسے ایمان پر موت دے۔ اے اللہ! ہمیں اس کے اجر و ثواب سے محروم نہ فرما اور اس کے بعد ہمیں گمراہ نہ کر۔

2. Authentic Dua for a Deceased Minor Child

AUTHENTIC
Citations: Sahih al-Bukhari (Ta'liqan, Kitab al-Jana'iz), Al-Bayhaqi (As-Sunan al-Kubra 4/9)
اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ لَنَا فَرَطًا، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا أَجْرًا وَذُخْرًا، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا شَافِعًا وَمُشَفَّعًا.

Grammar Note for Girls: If the deceased child is female, recite with feminine pronouns: اجْعَلْهَا لَنَا فَرَطًا، وَاجْعَلْهَا لَنَا أَجْرًا وَذُخْرًا، وَاجْعَلْهَا لَنَا شَافِعَةً وَمُشَفَّعَةً.

Allāhummaj-‘alhu lanā faraṭan, waj-‘alhu lanā ajran wa dhukhran, waj-‘alhu lanā shāfi‘an wa mushaffa‘ā.

English Translation: O Allah, make him for us an advance herald (a forerunner awaiting us in Paradise), make him for us a source of reward and accumulated treasure, and make him for us an accepted intercessor.

اے اللہ! اس بچے کو ہمارے لیے آگے بھیجا ہوا سامان بنا، اور اس کو ہمارے لیے اجر اور ذخیرہ بنا، اور اس کو ہمارے حق میں سفارش کرنے والا اور مقبول شفاعت بنا۔

5. Comparative Juristic (Madhhab) Summary

All four orthodox Sunni schools of jurisprudence agree on the core structure of Janazah. The minor procedural nuances are legitimate scholarly interpretations grounded in authentic hadith evidence:

Issue / Element Hanafi Shafi'i Maliki Hanbali
After 1st Takbir Thana (سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ); Fatihah as dua Surah Al-Fatihah is an obligatory pillar (Rukn) Praise of Allah; Fatihah is recommended Surah Al-Fatihah is an obligatory pillar (Rukn)
Raising Hands (Raf' al-Yadayn) First Takbir only In all 4 Takbirs (Sunnah) First Takbir only (famous view) In all 4 Takbirs (Sunnah)
After 2nd Takbir Durood Ibrahim (Wajib/Sunnah) Durood on the Prophet ﷺ (Rukn) Durood on the Prophet ﷺ (Sunnah) Durood Ibrahim (Rukn)
After 3rd Takbir Dua for deceased (Sunnah Mu'akkadah) Dua for deceased is an essential pillar (Rukn) Dua for deceased is the primary pillar (Rukn) Dua for deceased is an essential pillar (Rukn)
Tasleem (Exit) Two Tasleems (right and left) One Tasleem to right is Rukn; 2nd is Sunnah One Tasleem to right One Tasleem to right is the standard Sunnah

6. Edge Cases & Special Scenarios

Salat al-Gha'ib (Funeral Prayer in Absentia)

When the Christian King of Abyssinia, the Negus (Ashamah An-Najashi), embraced Islam and died without an Islamic community to pray over him, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ assembled his companions in Madinah and offered funeral prayer over him from afar (Sahih al-Bukhari #1317).
• Juristic Rule: Contemporary scholars generally agree that Salat al-Gha'ib is praiseworthy when a notable Muslim leader, scholar, or victim of disaster dies in a place where no Janazah was performed, or as a communal sign of honor for prominent figures.

Stillborn Infant or Miscarriage (Siqt)

• If the fetus was miscarried after 120 days (4 lunar months) of pregnancy, the soul had been breathed into it (Sahih al-Bukhari #3208). It is washed, shrouded, named, and prayed over with the child's Janazah dua.
• If miscarried prior to 120 days, it is gently wrapped in a clean cloth and buried with respect without a formal Janazah prayer.

Multiple Deceased in a Single Prayer

In instances of multiple casualties, war, or natural disasters, all bodies may be aligned side by side between the Imam and the Qiblah. The corpses of adult men are placed closest to the Imam, followed by boys, then adult women, and girls closest to the Qiblah. A single Janazah suffices for all.

7. Cemetery Etiquette, Burial Protocols & Sunnah Condolence

Entering a cemetery is an occasion for quiet introspection, reverence, and earnest prayer for the departed:

Dua Upon Entering the Graveyard (Ziyarat al-Qubur)

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ أَهْلَ الدِّيَارِ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ بِكُمْ لَاحِقُونَ، نَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ لَنَا وَلَكُمُ الْعَافِيَةَ.

As-salāmu ‘alaykum ahlad-diyāri minal-mu’mineena wal-muslimeen, wa innā in shā’ Allāhu bikum lāḥiqūn, nas’alullāha lanā wa lakumul-‘āfiyah.

English Meaning: Peace be upon you, O inhabitants of these dwellings from among the believers and Muslims. And indeed, when Allah wills, we shall join you. We ask Allah for well-being for us and for you. (Sahih Muslim #975)

سلامتی ہو تم پر اے بستی والوں، ایمان والوں اور مسلمانوں میں سے! اور بے شک ہم اگر اللہ نے چاہا تو تم سے ملنے والے ہیں، ہم اللہ سے اپنے لیے اور تمہارے لیے عافیت کے طلبگار ہیں۔

Sunnah Words of Condolence (Ta'ziyah) to the Bereaved

إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا أَخَذَ، وَلَهُ مَا أَعْطَىٰ، وَكُلُّ شَيْءٍ عِنْدَهُ بِأَجَلٍ مُسَمًّى، فَلْتَصْبِرْ وَلْتَحْتَسِبْ.

Inna lillāhi mā akhadha, wa lahū mā a‘ṭā, wa kullu shay’in ‘indahū bi-ajalim-musammā, fal-taṣbir wal-taḥtasib.

English Meaning: Truly, to Allah belongs what He took, and to Him belongs what He gave, and everything with Him has an appointed time; so be patient and hope for Allah's reward. (Sahih al-Bukhari #1284, Sahih Muslim #923)

8. Common Mistakes to Avoid in Janazah

❌ 1. Bowing (Ruku') or Prostrating (Sujud)

Salat al-Janazah is completely devoid of bowing and prostrating. Doing so invalidates the prayer.

❌ 2. Praying Without Proper Wudu

Some assume that because Janazah consists of standing and supplication, Wudu is unnecessary. Janazah is a formal prayer (Salah) requiring full ritual purity from minor and major impurities.

❌ 3. Wailing, Screaming, or Tearing Clothes (Niyāḥah)

While silent tears and heartfelt sorrow are natural and merciful, audible wailing, striking cheeks, and tearing collars are strictly forbidden by the Prophet ﷺ (Sahih al-Bukhari #1294).

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Salat al-Janazah obligatory on every individual?

No, it is a communal obligation (Fard Kifayah). If a group of Muslims performs it, the obligation is discharged for the entire community. However, attending carries enormous divine reward (two Qirats, each like Mount Uhud).

What is the ruling on praying Janazah in absentia (Salat al-Gha'ib)?

The Hanbali and Shafi'i schools permit it generally for anyone who passed away in another city. The Hanafi and Maliki schools hold that it was unique for the Prophet ﷺ or specifically permitted when no funeral prayer was offered over the deceased in the land where they died.

How is the Janazah prayer offered for a miscarried or stillborn fetus?

If the fetus completed four months (120 days) of pregnancy, it must be washed, shrouded, named, and prayed over with the child's Janazah dua. If miscarried before 120 days, it is wrapped in clean cloth and buried respectfully without a formal prayer.

Can women attend and pray Salat al-Janazah?

Yes. Women are permitted to pray Salat al-Janazah behind men in congregation or individually. What is discouraged is accompanying the bier all the way into the graveyard during burial.

What should a latecomer do if they arrive during Salat al-Janazah?

Join the Imam immediately by saying Takbir al-Ihram. When the Imam delivers the final Tasleem, quickly make up the missed Takbirs and their respective recitations before saying Salam.

Are shoes allowed during Salat al-Janazah?

Yes, provided the soles and tops of the shoes are completely clean of physical impurity (Najasah). If in doubt when praying on dirt or gravel, slip out of your shoes and stand on top of them.

Can Janazah be prayed inside a mosque?

Yes, it is permissible to pray Salat al-Janazah inside a mosque according to the Shafi'i, Hanbali, and famous narration of Aisha (RA) in Sahih Muslim #973. In the Hanafi school, praying in a designated outdoor funeral area (Musalla al-Jana'iz) is preferred when available.
