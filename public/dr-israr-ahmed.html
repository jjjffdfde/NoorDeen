How to use this library

1. Choose a series

Arbaeen-e-Nawawi explains forty foundational hadiths. Al-Huda is a structured study course through selected surahs. Muntakhab Nisab covers essential Islamic studies.

2. Follow the episode order

Each card shows its verified series code and episode number. Sorting is by series and episode, not by upload date, so the study sequence stays intact.

3. Read alongside the lecture

Pair a lecture with our hadith sciences guide, translation vs tafsir article, or the Quran reader.

4. Verify at the source

Every card links to the original Tanzeem-e-Islami page. If a video is removed or made private by its owner, the source link remains the authoritative record.
