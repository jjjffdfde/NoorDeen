The Noble Sahaba (رضي الله عنهم)

The Rightly Guided Caliphs (Khulafa ar-Rashidun) and Companions who preserved, lived, and conveyed the message of Islam to all generations. "And the first forerunners [in the faith] among the Muhajirin and the Ansar... Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him." (Surah At-Tawbah 9:100).

Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (رضي الله عنه)

First Caliph of Islam (11–13 AH) • Companion in the Cave • The Verifier of Truth
أبو بكر الصديق

The foremost companion of the Prophet ﷺ and the first adult male to accept Islam without hesitation. He spent his entire wealth freeing persecuted slaves (including Bilal ibn Rabah), accompanied the Prophet ﷺ in the Cave of Thawr during the dangerous Hijrah, unified the Ummah after the Prophet's passing, and commissioned the historic compilation of the Holy Quran into a single verified Mushaf under Zayd ibn Thabit.

"Whoever worshipped Muhammad, know that Muhammad has passed away; but whoever worships Allah, know that Allah is Ever-Living and does not die."

Umar ibn al-Khattab (رضي الله عنه)

Second Caliph (13–23 AH) • Al-Faruq (The Criterion Between Truth & Falsehood)
عمر بن الخطاب

His conversion granted the early Muslims the courage to pray openly at the Ka'bah. Renowned for legendary justice, incorruptible leadership, and egalitarian governance. He established the public welfare treasury (Bayt al-Mal), the Islamic postal service, military registers, the lunar Hijri calendar, and granted the historic Treaty of Jerusalem protecting Christian places of worship and civil rights.

"Since when did you enslave the people, when their mothers gave birth to them as free human beings?"

Uthman ibn Affan (رضي الله عنه)

Third Caliph (23–35 AH) • Dhun-Nurayn (Possessor of the Two Lights)
عثمان بن عفان

Renowned for extraordinary modesty (Haya'), selflessness, and boundless generosity. He financed the expansion of Masjid an-Nabawi, equipped the expedition to Tabuk, and purchased the Well of Rumah for free public water access in Madinah. In his caliphate, he standardized the official copies of the Holy Quran across all provinces, safeguarding the unified Hafs Uthmanic recitation for all eternity.

"If our hearts were truly pure, they would never tire of reciting the words of our Lord."

Ali ibn Abi Talib (رضي الله عنه)

Fourth Caliph (35–40 AH) • Gate to the City of Knowledge • Asadullah
علي بن أبي طالب

The first youth to embrace Islam. Cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet ﷺ, married to Sayyidah Fatimah az-Zahra, and father of Hasan and Husayn. Celebrated for heroic bravery on the battlefield, profound juristic wisdom, and unmatched mastery of Arabic eloquence, legal arbitration, and deep ascetic devotion.

"People are of two kinds: either your brother in faith, or your equal in creation."
