Sources, Methodology & Attribution

Complete transparency regarding source licensing, scholarly references, and data provenance.

1. Holy Quranic Text & Audio

Arabic Text: Sourced from the globally audited Tanzil Project and King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex in Madinah al-Munawwarah. The text adheres to the verified Uthmani script with authentic diacritics (Harakat) and standardized verse breaks.

Audio Recitation: High-definition audio streams courtesy of EveryAyah and Quran Central, featuring renowned Qaris including Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Sheikh Abdul Basit Abdul Samad, Sheikh Abu Bakr Ash-Shatri, and Sheikh Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary.

Translations:

  • English: Saheeh International (Abul A'la Maududi, Umm Muhammad, Aminah Assami) & The Clear Quran (Dr. Mustafa Khattab).
  • Urdu: Maulana Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry & Tafheem-ul-Quran (Syed Abul A'la Maududi).
  • Indonesian: Indonesian Ministry of Religious Affairs (Kemenag RI).
  • Turkish: Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet İşleri Başkanlığı).
  • French: Dr. Muhammad Hamidullah.

2. Hadith Collections & Gradings

Hadith narrations are drawn from the canonical six books of the Sunni tradition (Kutub al-Sittah):

  • Sahih al-Bukhari — Verified text and numbering based on the standard scholarly system.
  • Sahih Muslim — Complete authentic numbering and sanad references.
  • Jami` at-Tirmidhi — Scholarly gradings explicitly attributed to Imam at-Tirmidhi and Sheikh Muhammad Nasiruddin al-Albani.
  • Sunan Abi Dawud, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah — Verified numbering.

3. Duas & Supplications

Daily supplications, morning and evening adhkar, and situational prayers are extracted from Hisnul Muslim (The Fortress of the Muslim from the Invocations of the Quran and Sunnah) compiled by the respected scholar Dr. Sa'id bin Ali bin Wahf Al-Qahtani (رحمه الله).

4. Seerah & Prophetic History

Historical narratives and chronological milestones are derived from Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum (The Sealed Nectar) by Sheikh Safiur-Rahman Mubarakpuri (honored with 1st prize by the Muslim World League), As-Seerah an-Nabawiyyah by Ibn Hisham, and Al-Bidayah wan-Nihayah by Ibn Kathir.

5. Astronomical Prayer Times Mathematics

Calculations for astronomical prayer times employ established solar orbital trigonometric equations (solar declination, equation of time, solar altitude angles, and refraction corrections) matching algorithms recognized by the Muslim World League (MWL), Islamic Society of North America (ISNA), and Umm al-Qura University.
