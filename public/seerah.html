السِّيرَةُ النَّبَوِيَّةُ الشَّرِيفَةُ — THE NOBLE PROPHETIC BIOGRAPHY

The Prophetic Biography (As-Seerah)

The noble life, exemplary moral character, and eternal mercy of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum). "And We have not sent you, [O Muhammad], except as a mercy to the worlds." (Surah Al-Anbiya 21:107).

ERA 1: Pre-Islamic & Early Life (570 CE – 595 CE) النسب الشريف والمولد المبارك

Blessed Lineage, Birth, and Childhood

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born on Monday, 12th of Rabi al-Awwal in the Year of the Elephant (570 CE), into the noble Hashimite clan of Quraysh in Makkah. His father Abdullah passed away before his birth. He was nursed by Halimah as-Sa'diyyah in the desert, where he acquired pure Arabic speech and robust health. Even in his youth, he was renowned among all tribes as Al-Sadiq (The Truthful) and Al-Amin (The Trustworthy).

Key Historical Milestones: Year of the Elephant • Care by Halimah, Aminah & Abdul Muttalib • Protection of uncle Abu Talib • Hilf al-Fudul (Pact of the Virtuous) for social justice.
ERA 2: Makkan Period (610 CE) بدء الوحي في غار حراء

The First Revelation in Cave Hira

As he approached forty, the Prophet ﷺ loved solitude and contemplation in Cave Hira atop Jabal an-Nur. In Ramadan of 610 CE, Archangel Jibreel appeared and commanded: "Iqra'!" (Read!). Jibreel recited Surah Al-Alaq (96:1–5). Returning with trembling awe, his noble wife Khadijah (RA) comforted him with immortal words: "Never! By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you; you keep ties of kinship, help the poor, support the destitute, honor your guests, and assist those afflicted by calamities."

Key Historical Milestones: Descent of Surah Al-Alaq 1–5 • Reassurance by Sayyidah Khadijah (RA) • Confirmation by the scholar Waraqah ibn Nawfal • Beginning of Prophethood.
ERA 3: Makkan Period (619 CE – 621 CE) عام الحزن والإسراء والمعراج

The Year of Sorrow & Al-Isra wal-Mi'raj

Following the passing of his beloved wife Khadijah (RA) and his protective uncle Abu Talib, and severe persecution and stonings at Ta'if, Allah honored His Messenger with the miraculous Night Journey (Al-Isra) from Masjid al-Haram to Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem, and the Heavenly Ascension (Al-Mi'raj) through the seven heavens into the divine presence, where the gift of the five daily prayers was established.

Key Historical Milestones: Supplication of mercy for the people of Ta'if • Al-Isra to Jerusalem • Ascension through seven heavens • Direct gift of the 5 Daily Prayers (Salah).
ERA 4: Madinan Period (622 CE) الهجرة النبوية المباركة

The Great Migration (Hijrah) to Madinah

Accompanied by his loyal friend Abu Bakr (RA), the Prophet ﷺ migrated safely to Madinah after three nights in Cave Thawr. In Madinah, he constructed Masjid an-Nabawi, instituted sacred brotherhood (Muwakhah) between the Muhajirun and Ansar, and drafted the Constitution of Madinah—the world's first written constitutional treaty guaranteeing equal civic rights, mutual defense, and freedom of worship.

Key Historical Milestones: Miraculous protection in Cave Thawr • Building Masjid an-Nabawi • Constitution of Madinah (Mithaq al-Madinah).
ERA 5: Farewell & Eternal Legacy (630 CE – 632 CE) فتح مكة وحجة الوداع

The Conquest of Makkah & The Farewell Sermon

In 630 CE (8 AH), the Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah in peaceful triumph without vengeance, destroying 360 idols around the Ka'bah and declaring universal amnesty to his erstwhile persecutors: "Go, for you are free." During the Farewell Pilgrimage at Arafat, he delivered the historic Universal Human Rights Sermon, proclaiming the sanctity of human life, racial equality, and women's rights: "An Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab nor a non-Arab has any superiority over an Arab... except by piety (Taqwa)."

Key Historical Milestones: Bloodless conquest of Makkah • Farewell Sermon at Mount Arafat • Divine perfection of Islam (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:3) • Passing of the Prophet ﷺ in 11 AH.
