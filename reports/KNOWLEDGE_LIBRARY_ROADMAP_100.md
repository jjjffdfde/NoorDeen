## 3. Implementation Tracking & Editorial Governance

- **Publication Phasing:** Batches of 5 to 10 articles each.
- **Content Criteria per Topic:** Minimum 1,800 to 2,500 words, structured Table of Contents, semantic H2/H3 headings, full vocalized Arabic texts (Tashkeel), verified Hadith citations with collections and numbers, balanced presentation of recognized Madhhabs, Schema.org `FAQPage` and `BreadcrumbList` markup, and zero unverified claims.
- **Quality Assurance:** 100% automated test coverage in `test/run-tests.js` before release.
