Sources, Methodology & Attribution

Complete transparency regarding source licensing, scholarly references, authentic Hadith chains, and academic data provenance.

1. Holy Quranic Text, Translations & Recitations

Immutable Arabic Text: Sourced from the globally recognized Tanzil Project and King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex in Madinah al-Munawwarah. The text adheres strictly to the canonical Hafs 'an 'Asim recitation in the Uthmani script with authentic diacritics (Harakat) and 6,236 standardized verses.

Audio Recitations: High-definition audio streams courtesy of the EveryAyah Public Domain Audio Repository, featuring world-renowned Qaris:

2. Hadith Collections & Authenticity Gradings

Hadith narrations are drawn from the canonical six books of the Sunni tradition (Kutub al-Sittah):

  • Sahih al-Bukhari: Verified text and numbering based on the standard scholarly system of Muhammad Fu'ad Abdul Baqi.
  • Sahih Muslim: Complete authentic numbering, book divisions, and verified sanad references.
  • Jami` at-Tirmidhi: Scholarly gradings explicitly attributed to Imam at-Tirmidhi, Hafiz Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani, and Sheikh Muhammad Nasiruddin al-Albani.
  • Sunan Abi Dawud, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Ibn Majah: Verified canonical numbering.

3. Supplications (Duas) & Daily Adhkar

Daily supplications, morning and evening adhkar, post-prayer invocations, and situational supplications are extracted from Hisnul Muslim (The Fortress of the Muslim from the Invocations of the Quran and Sunnah) compiled by the esteemed scholar Dr. Sa'id bin Ali bin Wahf Al-Qahtani (رحمه الله), verified against original citations in Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, and Tirmidhi.

4. Seerah & Prophetic History

Historical narratives and chronological milestones are derived from Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum (The Sealed Nectar) by Sheikh Safiur-Rahman Mubarakpuri (awarded 1st prize worldwide by the Muslim World League), As-Seerah an-Nabawiyyah by Ibn Hisham, and Al-Bidayah wan-Nihayah by Hafiz Ibn Kathir.

5. Astronomical Solar Mathematics & Qibla Trigonometry

Calculations for astronomical prayer times employ established solar orbital trigonometric equations (solar declination, equation of time, solar altitude angles, and atmospheric refraction corrections) matching algorithms codified by the Muslim World League (MWL), Islamic Society of North America (ISNA), and Umm al-Qura University in Makkah. Qibla bearing utilizes great-circle forward azimuth navigation toward the Holy Ka'bah (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E).
