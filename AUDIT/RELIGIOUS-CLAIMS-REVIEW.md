## 1. Principles of Theological Classification

To prevent common misrepresentations where traditional pious practices are conflated with binding Prophetic mandates, the Islamic Knowledge Portal enforces a three-tier theological classification system:

1. **Tier 1: Confirmed Sahih Sunnah (سنة صحيحة ثابتة)**
   - Traditions with continuous, authentic isnad chains meeting the criteria of Imam al-Bukhari, Imam Muslim, or authentic gradings from leading Hadith critics (Ibn Hajar, Adh-Dhahabi, An-Nawawi, Al-Albani).
   - Binding devotional recommendations with established Prophetic precedent.
2. **Tier 2: Authentic / Sound Sunnah (سنة حسنة)**
   - Traditions graded Hasan by authoritative scholars (such as Imam at-Tirmidhi). Fully acceptable as established Sunnah.
3. **Tier 3: Permissible Pious Practice / Virtues of Deeds (مستحب / عمل السلف / فضائل الأعمال)**
   - Traditions containing historical weakness in their specific transmission chain (Mursal or Da'if), but whose practice is well-attested among early righteous predecessors (*Amal al-Salaf*) and supported by universal Quranic exhortations to recite and reflect upon Allah's words.
   - Distinctly labeled so the seeker understands they are virtuous voluntary practices rather than binding religious mandates.
