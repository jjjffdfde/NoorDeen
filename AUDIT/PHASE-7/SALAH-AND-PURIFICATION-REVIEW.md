## 1. Executive Overview

Purification (*Taharah*) is the indispensable key to prayer (*Miftah as-Salah*), and Prayer (*Salah*) is the second and central pillar of Islam. The portal provides learners with a rigorous, accessible, step-by-step educational system for Wudu, Ghusl, Tayammum, and the five daily prayers.

This review verifies:
1. The structural integrity, sequence, and authentic recitations across `public/salah-guide.html` and `public/wudu-guide.html`.

3. The transparent and neutral presentation of established juristic variations (*Madhahib*) without partisan dogma or confusion.
4. Mobile usability, clear typography, and verifiable source citations from classical Hadith and Fiqh corpora.

## 2. Complete Purification System Audit (`public/wudu-guide.html`)

The purification module at `public/wudu-guide.html` (57,475 bytes) covers the full spectrum of minor and major ritual purity:

### 2.1 Wudu (Minor Ritual Ablution)
- **The Qur'anic Mandate**: Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6 clearly cited as the foundational constitutional verse of Islamic purification.
- **The 4 Obligatory (*Fard*) Acts**:
  1. Washing the face from forehead hairline to chin and ear to ear.
  2. Washing both arms including elbows.
  3. Wiping the head (*Masah* — full head in Maliki/Hanbali; at least one-quarter in Hanafi; a few hairs in Shafi'i).
  4. Washing both feet up to and including ankles.
- **The 14 Sunan Prophetic Acts**: Intention, Basmalah, washing hands to wrists 3x, Miswak/rinsing mouth 3x, inhaling water into nose 3x, running wet fingers through beard (*Takhlil al-Lihyah*), washing between fingers and toes, wiping ears, and maintaining proper sequence (*Tartib*) and continuity (*Muwalah*).
- **Post-Wudu Supplications**: *Ash-hadu alla ilaha illallah...* and *Allahummaj-'alni minat-tawwabeen...* (Sahih Muslim #234; Jami` at-Tirmidhi #55).
- **Wiping Over Socks (*Masah alal-Khuffayn*)**: Valid for 24 hours for resident, 72 hours for traveler upon leather/thick socks put on after complete Wudu.
- **Nullifiers (*Nawaqid*)**: Bodily emissions (urine, stool, wind, prostatic fluid), deep sleep lying down, loss of consciousness, flowing blood/pus (Hanafi).

### 2.2 Ghusl (Major Ritual Bath) — Newly Added Module
- **Quranic Basis**: Surah An-Nisa 4:43 and Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6 (*"And if you are in a state of Janabah, then purify yourselves thoroughly"*).
- **Obligatory Causes (*Wajibaat*)**:
  1. Emission of semen/fluid (*Mani*) with desire or climax, whether awake or asleep (wet dream).
  2. Sexual intercourse (penetration of private parts, even without ejaculation — Sahih Muslim #349).
  3. Cessation of menstruation (*Hayd*).
  4. Cessation of postnatal bleeding (*Nifas*).
  5. Conversion to Islam.
  6. Washing of a deceased Muslim (*Ghusl al-Mayyit*).
- **Sunnah Recommended Occasions**: Jumu'ah Friday prayer (Bukhari #877), the two Eids, entering Ihram for Hajj/Umrah, Day of Arafah.
- **Mandatory Pillars (*Fara'id*)**:
  1. Thorough mouth rinsing (*Madmadah*).
  2. Sniffing water into nostrils (*Istinshaq*).
  3. Washing the complete exterior body, ensuring water penetrates to the roots of all hair and skin folds.
- **Prophetic Method Step-by-Step**: As documented by Sayyidah Aishah (RA) in Sahih al-Bukhari #248 and Sahih Muslim #316:
  1. Intention (*Niyyah*) in the heart.
  2. Washing hands to wrists 3 times.
  3. Washing private parts with left hand.
  4. Performing complete Wudu as for prayer.
  5. Pouring water over head 3 times, rubbing roots of hair.
  6. Washing right side of body, then left side.
  7. Washing feet.

### 2.3 Tayammum (Dry Earth Ablution) — Newly Added Module
- **Quranic Basis**: Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6 & Surah An-Nisa 4:43 (*"Then perform Tayammum with clean earth and wipe over your faces and hands with it"*).
- **Legitimate Validating Excuses**: Total absence of water after reasonable search; medical inability/wound harm; severe freezing cold with risk of illness; preserving life-saving drinking water; imminent physical danger.
- **The Clean Earth Medium (*Sa'id Tayyib*)**: Pure natural soil, rock, sand, unglazed stone.
- **Sequential Method**:
  1. Intention in the heart.
  2. Saying *Bismillah*.
  3. Striking clean earth/stone once with palms spread.
  4. Blowing off excess dust.
  5. Wiping the face once.
  6. Wiping the back of the right hand with left palm, and back of left hand with right palm (Ammar ibn Yasir narration in Sahih al-Bukhari #338 and Sahih Muslim #368; Hanafi/Shafi'i jurists permit wiping to elbows).
- **Nullifiers**: Any nullifier of Wudu/Ghusl; the arrival or availability of clean water; the healing of the injury or medical impediment.

## 3. Complete Daily Prayer Guide Audit (`public/salah-guide.html`)

The guide at `public/salah-guide.html` (76,042 bytes) provides a master curriculum for beginner and intermediate worshippers:

### 3.1 Prerequisites & Preparation (*Shurut al-Salah*)
1. Purity from major and minor impurities (Wudu / Ghusl / Tayammum).
2. Cleanliness of clothing, body, and prayer area from physical filth (*Najasah*).
3. Covering the *Awrah* (men: navel to knee at minimum; women: entire body except face and hands).
4. Entry of the prescribed prayer time (*Dukhul al-Waqt*).
5. Facing the direction of the Ka'bah (*Istiqbal al-Qiblah*).
6. Sincere Intention (*Niyyah*) in the heart.

### 3.2 Daily 5 Prayers Rak'ah Breakdown Table
| Prayer | Sunnah Mu'akkadah (Before) | Fard (Obligatory) | Sunnah Mu'akkadah (After) | Nafl / Witr |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fajr (Dawn)** | 2 Rak'ahs | 2 Rak'ahs (Audible) | — | — |
| **Dhuhr (Noon)** | 4 Rak'ahs | 4 Rak'ahs (Silent) | 2 Rak'ahs | 2 Nafl (Opt.) |
| **Asr (Afternoon)** | 4 Ghayr Mu'akkadah (Opt.) | 4 Rak'ahs (Silent) | — | — |
| **Maghrib (Sunset)** | — | 3 Rak'ahs (1-2 Audible, 3 Silent) | 2 Rak'ahs | 2 Nafl (Opt.) |
| **Isha (Night)** | 4 Ghayr Mu'akkadah (Opt.) | 4 Rak'ahs (1-2 Audible, 3-4 Silent) | 2 Rak'ahs | 2 Nafl + 3 Witr |

### 3.3 Step-by-Step Postures & Authentic Recitations
1. **Takbir al-Ihram**: Facing Qiblah, raising hands to ears (Hanafi) or shoulders (Shafi'i/Hanbali), reciting *Allahu Akbar*.
2. **Qiyam (Standing)**: Hands placed below navel (Hanafi) or on chest (Shafi'i/Hanbali).
   - Reciting the opening supplication (*Thana*): `سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَىٰ جَدُّكَ، وَلَا إِلَٰهَ غَيْرُكَ` (Abu Dawud #776).
   - Ta'awwudh & Basmalah.
   - Surah Al-Fatihah followed by Ameen.
   - Recitation of an additional Surah (in first two Rak'ahs).
3. **Ruku' (Bowing)**: Back parallel to the ground, hands grasping knees with fingers spread, reciting 3 times: `سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ` (*Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azeem* — Sahih Muslim #772).
4. **Qawmah (Standing Upright)**: Rising saying `سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ` (*Sami'allahu liman hamidah*), followed upright by `رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ، حَمْدًا كَثِيرًا طَيِّبًا مُبَارَكًا فِيهِ` (Sahih al-Bukhari #799).
5. **Sujud (Prostration)**: Prostrating upon 7 limbs (forehead with nose, both palms, both knees, and toes of both feet facing Qiblah). Reciting 3 times: `سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَىٰ` (*Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la* — Sahih Muslim #772).
6. **Jalsah (Sitting between Sujud)**: Sitting upright in tranquility (*Tuma'neenah*), reciting: `رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي، رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي، وَارْحَمْنِي، وَاهْدِنِي، وَارْزُقْنِي` (Sunan Abi Dawud #850).
7. **Second Sujud**: Exactly identical to the first.
8. **Tashahhud (Sitting for Testimony)**: Reciting the Ibn Mas'ud (RA) formulation: `التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ...` (Sahih al-Bukhari #831; Sahih Muslim #402).
9. **Durood Ibrahimiyyah**: Sending blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ: `اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ...` (Sahih al-Bukhari #3370).
10. **Supplication Before Tasleem**: Seeking refuge from 4 trials: `اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ جَهَنَّمَ، وَمِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ، وَمِنْ فِتْنَةِ الْمَحْيَا وَالْمَمَاتِ، وَمِنْ شَرِّ فِتْنَةِ الْمَسِيحِ الدَّجَّالِ` (Sahih Muslim #588).
11. **Tasleem (Concluding Salutation)**: Turning head to the right saying `السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ`, then to the left.

### 3.4 Madhhab Comparative Rigor
The guide maintains scholarly neutrality across the recognized schools:
- **Raf' al-Yadayn**: Performed only at opening Takbir in the Hanafi school (based on Ibn Mas'ud narration); performed also before bowing, rising from bowing, and standing after the first Tashahhud in Shafi'i and Hanbali schools (based on Ibn Umar narration in Bukhari #735).
- **Recitation Behind Imam**: In loud prayers, Hanafi worshippers listen silently (based on Surah Al-A'raf 7:204); Shafi'i worshippers recite Surah Al-Fatihah in silence even behind the Imam (based on Bukhari #756).
- **Witr Prayer**: Hanafi school observes 3 continuous Rak'ahs with one final Tasleem, raising hands and reciting Qunut before Ruku' in the 3rd Rak'ah; Shafi'i and Hanbali schools prefer 2 Rak'ahs with Tasleem followed by 1 separate Rak'ah with Qunut recited standing after Ruku'.

## 4. Special Worship Scenarios
- **Latecomer (*Masbuq*)**: Clear guidance on how to join the congregation at any posture, determining Rak'ahs caught (catching Ruku' counts as catching the Rak'ah), and completing missed units after the Imam finishes.
- **Traveler Prayer (*Qasr & Jam'*)**: Shortening 4-Rak'ah prayers to 2 Rak'ahs when traveling beyond the Shar'i travel distance (approx. 77–88 km / 48 miles), and combining Dhuhr with Asr, and Maghrib with Isha when on the journey.
- **Praying on a Chair (*Salat al-Marid*)**: Precise guidelines for elderly, injured, or sick worshippers—standing if able, bowing normally if able, and making prostrations with head lower than the bow without placing artificial pillows or tables in front.
- **Prostrations of Forgetfulness (*Sujud al-Sahwi*)**: Remedying unintentional omissions of obligatory acts (*Wajibaat*) or doubts about the number of Rak'ahs by performing two prostrations before or after Tasleem.
