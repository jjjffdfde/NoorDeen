## 1. Executive Summary

Salat al-Janazah (the Islamic funeral prayer) and the two Eid prayers (Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha) are among the most communal, spiritually poignant, and distinct worship assemblies in Islam. They feature unique procedural structures that diverge sharply from standard daily prayers:
- **Janazah Prayer**: Performed entirely in the standing posture (*Qiyam*) with **FOUR TAKBIRS**, containing **ABSOLUTELY ZERO RUKU' (BOWING) AND ZERO SUJUD (PROSTRATION)**.
- **Eid Prayers**: Two Rak'ahs incorporating additional glorification Takbirs (*Zawa'id Takbirs*), with the Khutbah (sermon) delivered *after* the prayer rather than before it.

This audit validates `public/janazah-guide.html` (55,227 bytes) and `public/eid-guide.html` (42,794 bytes) for canonical accuracy, safeguarding against errors, Madhhab transparency, and accessibility.

## 2. Funeral Prayer Audit (`public/janazah-guide.html`)

### 2.1 Communal Obligation (*Fard Kifayah*)
The funeral prayer is classified as a communal obligation (*Fard Kifayah*). If a sufficient group of Muslims performs it, the obligation is discharged on behalf of the entire community; if abandoned entirely, the whole community bears sin.

### 2.2 Spatial Alignment & Biomechanics
The portal correctly documents the authentic positioning of the Imam relative to the deceased:
- **Male Deceased (*Mayyit*)**: The Imam stands aligned directly opposite the head or chest of the deceased (Sahih al-Bukhari #1331; Sunan Abi Dawud #3194).
- **Female Deceased (*Mayyitah*)**: The Imam stands aligned opposite the midsection / waist of the bier (Sunan Abi Dawud #3194, narrated by Anas ibn Malik RA).

### 2.3 The Four Takbirs Sequence Step-by-Step
The guide reinforces the absolute safeguard: **NO RUKU' AND NO SUJUD**.
1. **First Takbir (Takbir al-Tahrimah)**:
   - Imam and congregation raise hands to ears/shoulders and fold hands over chest/navel.
   - Silent recitation of *Thana* (`سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ...`) or Surah Al-Fatihah (obligatory in Shafi'i/Hanbali schools based on Ibn Abbas RA in Sahih al-Bukhari #1335).
2. **Second Takbir**:
   - Hands raised (or kept folded according to school).
   - Recitation of *Durood Ibrahimiyyah* upon the Prophet ﷺ (`اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ...`).
3. **Third Takbir**:
   - The central purpose of the prayer: heartfelt supplication for the deceased.
   - **For Adult Deceased**:
     `اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِحَيِّنَا وَمَيِّتِنَا، وَشَاهِدِنَا وَغَائِبِنَا، وَصَغِيرِنَا وَكَبِيرِنَا، وَذَكَرِنَا وَأُنْثَانَا...` (Sunan Abi Dawud #3201; Jami` at-Tirmidhi #1024 — Sahih).
   - **For Deceased Child (*Tifl / Siqt*)**:
     `اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ لَنَا فَرَطًا، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا أَجْرًا وَذُخْرًا، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا شَافِعًا وَمُشَفَّعًا` (Sahih al-Bukhari Ta'liqan; Al-Bayhaqi in As-Sunan al-Kubra 4/9).
4. **Fourth Takbir**:
   - A brief general supplication followed immediately by Tasleem.
   - **Tasleem**: One audible Tasleem to the right (Sunnah in Hanbali/Shafi'i/Maliki) or two Tasleems to both sides (Hanafi school).

### 2.4 Edge Cases & Cemetery Etiquette
- **Salat al-Gha'ib (Prayer in Absentia)**: Legitimate when a Muslim dies in a distant region where no funeral prayer was offered over them, following the Prophet's ﷺ action for the Negus of Abyssinia (Sahih al-Bukhari #1318).
- **Miscarriage (*Siqt*)**: If an embryo is miscarried after 4 months (120 days of gestation when the soul is breathed into it) and shows signs of life, it is named, washed, shrouded, and prayed over.
- **Strict Prohibition of Wailing (*Niyāḥah*)**: Tearing garments, beating cheeks, and screaming loud laments are categorically prohibited (Sahih al-Bukhari #1294). Quiet weeping and sorrow of the heart are natural and permissible.
- **Graveyard Dua (*Ziyarat al-Qubur*)**: `السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ أَهْلَ الدِّيَارِ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ...` (Sahih Muslim #975).

## 3. The Two Eids Guide Audit (`public/eid-guide.html`)

### 3.1 Spiritual Purpose & Timing
- **Eid al-Fitr (1st of Shawwal)**: Celebration of completing the obligatory fast of Ramadan; thanksgiving to Allah for spiritual renewal.
- **Eid al-Adha (10th of Dhul Hijjah)**: The Festival of Sacrifice honoring the supreme devotion of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) and coinciding with the culmination of Hajj rites at Mina and Makkah.
- **Timing**: Begins when the sun has risen above the horizon by a spear's length (approx. 15–20 minutes after sunrise) and extends until true solar midday (*Zawwal*).

### 3.2 Dual Madhhab Methods for Extra Takbirs (*Takbirat az-Zawa'id*)
The portal transparently details both classical Sunni methods to avoid congregational confusion:

#### Method 1: Hanafi Madhhab (6 Additional Takbirs)
- **First Rak'ah**:
  1. Opening Takbir al-Ihram.
  2. Recitation of silent *Thana*.
  3. **3 Additional Takbirs**: Hands raised each time. For the 1st and 2nd, hands are released to the sides; for the 3rd, hands are folded.
  4. Recitation of Surah Al-Fatihah and an additional Surah.
  5. Regular Ruku' and Sujud.
- **Second Rak'ah**:
  1. Recitation of Surah Al-Fatihah and additional Surah first.
  2. **3 Additional Takbirs**: Hands raised and released to the sides.
  3. 4th Takbir is the Takbir for Ruku' (bowing without raising hands).

#### Method 2: Shafi'i & Hanbali Madhhab (12 Additional Takbirs)
- **First Rak'ah**:
  1. Opening Takbir al-Ihram.
  2. **7 Additional Takbirs**: Hands raised with each, reciting silent Dhikr (*SubhanAllah, wal-hamdulillah, wa la ilaha illallah, wallahu Akbar*) between them.
  3. Recitation of Surah Al-Fatihah and additional Surah (Surah Qaf or Al-A'la).
  4. Ruku' and Sujud.
- **Second Rak'ah**:
  1. Takbir al-Qiyam (rising from Sujud).
  2. **5 Additional Takbirs** before recitation.
  3. Recitation of Surah Al-Fatihah and additional Surah (Surah Al-Qamar or Al-Ghashiyah).
  4. Ruku' and Sujud.

### 3.3 Takbirat al-Tashreeq Schedule
The portal establishes the exact 23-prayer schedule of Takbirat al-Tashreeq:
- **Commences**: After Fajr prayer on the 9th of Dhul Hijjah (Day of Arafah).
- **Concludes**: After Asr prayer on the 13th of Dhul Hijjah (the final day of Tashreeq).
- **Wording**: `اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ` (Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah #5633; Daruqutni).

### 3.4 Zakat al-Fitr & Qurbani (Udhiyyah)
- **Zakat al-Fitr**: Must be distributed to the poor before the commencement of the Eid al-Fitr prayer (one Sa' of staple food / cash equivalent) so the needy can celebrate without begging.
- **Qurbani / Udhiyyah**: Sacrificing a livestock animal (sheep/goat = 1 share; cattle/camel = up to 7 shares). Animals must be free of major defects (blindness, lameness, emaciation) and of minimum age (sheep 6-12 months, cattle 2 years, camel 5 years). Slaughter must occur *after* the Eid prayer.
