## Scoring Scale Definition
- **0 — Absent or completely broken:** Missing or unusable.
- **1 — Very weak:** Incomplete or highly unreliable.
- **2 — Needs substantial improvement:** Works in limited cases with major gaps.
- **3 — Competent:** Functional for core tasks with minor limitations.
- **4 — Strong:** Well-engineered, clear, and dependable.
- **5 — Excellent for defined purpose:** Exemplary implementation meeting rigorous criteria.
- **N/A — Not applicable:** Explicitly outside the declared mission or scope of that platform.
- **N/V — Not verified:** Insufficient independent evidence to assign a confident score.
