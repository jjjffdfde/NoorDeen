## 1. Editorial Methodology & Scholarly Sourcing Hierarchy

The biographical treatment of the Final Messenger of Allah, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, adheres strictly to the primary historical corpora recognized by the universal scholarly consensus (*Ijma'*) of Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah:

1. **Level 1 (Highest Authority — Muttafaqun 'Alayh):** Traditions narrated simultaneously in *Sahih al-Bukhari* and *Sahih Muslim* with unbroken, rigorous isnad chains.
2. **Level 2 (Canonical Sunan Corpora):** Authentic narratives recorded in *Sunan Abi Dawud*, *Jami' at-Tirmidhi*, *Sunan an-Nasa'i*, *Sunan Ibn Majah*, and *Musnad Ahmad*.
3. **Level 3 (Foundational Classical Seerah Texts):**
   - Muhammad ibn Ishaq (d. 151 AH): *Al-Maghazi / As-Seerah*.
   - Abd al-Malik ibn Hisham (d. 218 AH): *As-Seerah an-Nabawiyyah*.
   - Muhammad ibn Sa'd (d. 230 AH): *At-Tabaqat al-Kubra*.
   - Al-Hafiz Ibn Kathir (d. 774 AH): *Al-Bidayah wan-Nihayah* (As-Seerah an-Nabawiyyah).
   - Safiur Rahman Mubarakpuri: *Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum* (The Sealed Nectar — First Prize World Muslim League).
