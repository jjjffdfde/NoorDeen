## 2. Biographical Inventory & Classical Citations

### Section 1: The Four Rightly Guided Caliphs
1. **Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Al-Siddiq (The Verifier of Truth), Companion of the Cave.
   - *Key Legacy:* Riddah wars unification, compilation of the Holy Quran into the first Mushaf under Zayd ibn Thabit.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3654, #4986; Sahih Muslim #2382; Ibn Hajar, *Al-Isabah* #4823.
2. **Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Al-Faruq (The Criterion Between Truth & Falsehood), Amir al-Mu'minin.
   - *Key Legacy:* Institutionalization of public welfare treasury (Bayt al-Mal), universal citizen pensions, the Hijri calendar, Treaty of Jerusalem.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3681; Sahih Muslim #2398; At-Tabari, *Tarikh* 4/240.
3. **Uthman ibn Affan (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Dhun-Nurayn (Possessor of the Two Lights).
   - *Key Legacy:* Equipping Jaysh al-'Usrah, Well of Rumah public trust, official standardization and distribution of the Uthmanic Mushaf.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3696, #4987; Sahih Muslim #2401; Ibn Sa'd 3/53–79.
4. **Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Gate to the City of Knowledge, Asadullah (The Lion of Allah).
   - *Key Legacy:* Heroic valor at Khaybar, supreme mastery of jurisprudence and Arabic eloquence, steadfast defense of egalitarian justice.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3706; Sahih Muslim #2404; Jami' at-Tirmidhi #3713; *Al-Isabah* #5690.

### Section 2: Mothers of the Believers & Eminent Sahabiyyat
5. **Khadijah bint Khuwaylid (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Mother of the Believers, Al-Tahirah (The Pure One), First Believer.
   - *Key Legacy:* Steadfast emotional and financial pillar of the early call, divine Salam delivered via Archangel Jibreel.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3815, #3821; Sahih Muslim #2434, #2435; Musnad Ahmad #24864.
6. **Aisha bint Abi Bakr (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Mother of the Believers, Al-Siddiqah, The Premier Jurist of Islam.
   - *Key Legacy:* Narrator of 2,210+ authentic Hadiths, master of medicine, inheritance laws, Arabic poetry, teacher of senior Sahaba.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3769, #3823; Sahih Muslim #2446; Jami' at-Tirmidhi #3883.
7. **Fatimah az-Zahra (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Sayyidatu Nisa' al-Jannah (Leader of the Women of Paradise), Umm Abiha.
   - *Key Legacy:* Purest reflection of the Prophet's character, mother of Al-Hasan and Al-Husayn, symbol of selfless maternal devotion.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3714, #3767; Sahih Muslim #2449; Jami' at-Tirmidhi #3878.
8. **Umm Salamah Hind bint Abi Umayyah (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Mother of the Believers, Sage of Hudaybiyyah.
   - *Key Legacy:* Immigrant to Abyssinia and Madinah, decisive diplomatic wisdom at Hudaybiyyah that saved the community from spiritual crisis.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #2731, #2732; Sahih Muslim #918; Ibn Hajar, *Tahdhib* 12/455.
9. **Asma bint Abi Bakr (RA):**
   - *Epithet:* Dhat an-Nitaqayn (Possessor of the Two Waistbands).
   - *Key Legacy:* Heroine of the Cave of Thawr supply lines during the Hijrah, mother of Abdullah ibn az-Zubayr, exemplar of unflinching courage before tyranny.
   - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #2979, #3905; Sahih Muslim #1005, #2540; Ibn Sa'd 8/249.

### Section 3: Illustrious Companions & Luminaries
10. **Bilal ibn Rabah (RA):**
    - *Epithet:* Mu'adhin of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, The Voice of Monotheism.
    - *Key Legacy:* Steadfastness under torture ("Ahad! Ahad!"), proclaimed Adhan from the Ka'bah, living embodiment of racial equality in Islam.
    - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3754, #1143; Sahih Muslim #2497; Ibn Hisham 1/317.
11. **Salman al-Farsi (RA):**
    - *Epithet:* The Seeker of Truth, Member of the Ahl al-Bayt.
    - *Key Legacy:* Journey across Zoroastrianism and Christianity to Islam, architectural strategist of the Trench at the Battle of the Ahzab.
    - *Primary Citations:* Musnad Ahmad #23737; Ibn Hisham 1/212–222; Al-Hakim, *Al-Mustadrak* 3/598.
12. **Khalid ibn al-Walid (RA):**
    - *Epithet:* Sayfullah al-Maslul (The Drawn Sword of Allah).
    - *Key Legacy:* Undefeated commander in 100+ battles, tactical triumph at Yarmouk, humble obedience when demoted to serve under Abu Ubaydah.
    - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3757, #4261; At-Tabari 3/395; Ibn Kathir, *Al-Bidayah* 7/115.
13. **Mus'ab ibn Umayr (RA):**
    - *Epithet:* First Ambassador of Islam, Mus'ab al-Khayr.
    - *Key Legacy:* Transformed aristocratic vanity into ultimate devotion, brought Madinah to Islam through gentle Quranic recitation, standard-bearer of Uhud.
    - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #1276, #3889; Sahih Muslim #940; Ibn Hisham 1/434.
14. **Abu Dharr al-Ghifari (RA):**
    - *Epithet:* Voice of Asceticism (Zuhd), Champion of the Oppressed.
    - *Key Legacy:* Fourth person to accept Islam, publicly proclaimed faith at the Ka'bah under violent assault, untainted truthfulness confirmed by the Prophet ﷺ.
    - *Primary Citations:* Sahih al-Bukhari #3522, #3861; Sahih Muslim #2473; Jami' at-Tirmidhi #3801.
