## 1. Executive Editorial Mission

The Islamic Knowledge Portal is committed to publishing authentic, beautifully articulated, and rigorously sourced Islamic educational content. Our mission is to educate Muslims and sincere seekers worldwide with clarity, reverence, and academic honesty.

These Editorial Standards govern all current and future publications across the website, mobile applications, and downloadable educational materials.

## 2. Article Architecture & Structural Standards

Every substantive educational article must adhere to a 10-part structural framework:

1. **Descriptive, Authentic Title:** Must accurately summarize the subject (e.g. *"Complete Step-by-Step Salah Guide: Postures, Recitations & Fiqh"*) without sensationalist clickbait.
2. **Contextual Introduction (Bismillah & Summary):** Opens with a concise executive summary stating who the subject is, why it matters spiritually, and what practical lessons the reader will acquire.
3. **Chronological / Logical Headings:** Divided cleanly using semantic Markdown/HTML headings (`<h2>`, `<h3>`).
4. **Primary Quranic & Prophetic Evidence:** Central claims must be anchored in divine revelation or authenticated Sunnah, presented in Arabic script with diacritics, clear transliteration, and English/Urdu meanings.
5. **Nuanced Juristic (Fiqh) Analysis:** Where recognized classical schools of thought (*Madhhabs*) differ, their positions must be presented with academic respect (*Adab al-Ikhtilaf*), explaining the linguistic or Hadith-based reasoning behind each stance.
6. **Historical Context & Provenance:** Events, battles, and treaties must include precise dates (both Hijri AH and Common Era CE), geographic locations, and primary classical historical citations.
7. **Practical Modern Application:** Bridges the 1,400-year historical gap by extracting actionable moral, spiritual, and ethical takeaways for contemporary life.
8. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ):** Addresses common misunderstandings and edge cases faced by learners and new Muslims.
9. **Academic Bibliography:** Explicitly lists the primary and secondary works consulted at the conclusion of the guide.
10. **Editorial Governance Badge:** Displays the current review status and verification classification.

## 4. Tone, Reverence & Accessibility

### 4.1. The Educational Tone
- **Calm, Dignified, and Reverent:** The voice of the portal is that of a knowledgeable, compassionate Islamic teacher. Avoid aggressive polemics, sectarian mockery, or sensationalist rhetoric.
- **Accessible to Beginners:** Define technical Arabic terminology upon first use (e.g. *"Wudu (minor ritual ablution)"*, *"Takbir (proclaiming Allahu Akbar)"*, *"Nisab (minimum zakat threshold)"*).
- **Spiritual Depth with Practical Clarity:** Balance inward spiritual devotion (*Ihsan*) with outward legal correctness (*Fiqh*).

### 4.2. Sacred Text Protection
- **Quranic Arabic Text:** Must **never** be rephrased, shortened, or modified for stylistic convenience. It must remain 100% faithful to the Hafs Uthmani script and wrapped with `lang="ar" dir="rtl" translate="no"`.
- **Transliteration Standard:** Follow the standard Library of Congress / American Library Association (ALA-LC) Arabic transliteration system:
  - Long vowels: *ā* (ـَا), *ī* (ـِي), *ū* (ـُو).
  - Specialized consonants: *ṣ* (ص), *ḍ* (ض), *ṭ* (ط), *ẓ* (ظ), *ḥ* (ح), *kh* (خ), *dh* (ذ), *sh* (ش), *'ayn* (ع), *hamzah* (ء).
- **Honorifics:** Use proper honorific typography:
  - Prophet Muhammad: ﷺ (*sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam*)
  - Other Prophets: (عليه السلام / *'alayhis-salam*)
  - Companions (Male): (رضي الله عنه / *radiyallahu 'anhu*)
  - Companions (Female): (رضي الله عنها / *radiyallahu 'anha*)
  - Companions (Plural): (رضي الله عنهم / *radiyallahu 'anhum*)

## 5. Standardized Citation & Reference Style

Every citation must provide enough bibliographic detail for a student or researcher to verify the reference in a standard Islamic library:

### 5.1. Quranic Citations:
Format: `Surah [English Name] [Surah Number]:[Ayah Number]`  
*Example:* `Surah Al-Baqarah 2:255` or `Surah Al-A'raf 7:23`.

### 5.2. Canonical Hadith Citations:
Format: `[Collection Name] #[Hadith Number] ([Grading], [Grading Authority])`  
*Examples:*
- `Sahih al-Bukhari #1 (Muttafaqun 'Alayh)`
- `Sahih Muslim #2564 (Sahih)`
- `Sunan Abi Dawud #1400 (Sahih, Shaykh al-Albani)`
- `Jami` at-Tirmidhi #1924 (Hasan Sahih, Imam at-Tirmidhi)`

### 5.3. Classical Historical Works:
Format: `[Author], [Book Title], ed. [Editor Name] ([City]: [Publisher], [Year]), [Volume]:[Page]`  
*Example:* `Ibn Hisham, Al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah, ed. Mustafa al-Saqqa (Cairo: Al-Babi al-Halabi, 1955), 1:240.`

### 5.4. Classical Biographical Works:
Format: `[Author], [Book Title], Entry #[Entry Number]`  
*Example:* `Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani, Al-Isabah fi Tamyiz al-Sahaba, Entry #8423 (Bilal ibn Rabah).`
