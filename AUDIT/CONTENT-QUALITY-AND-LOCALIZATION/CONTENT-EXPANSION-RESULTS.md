Key Expansion Highlights:

## 2. Sahaba Biography Expansion (52 Monographs)

In [`public/sahaba.html`](file:///e:/islamic%20websiye/public/sahaba.html), 22 newly authored scholarly monographs were integrated, bringing the total to 52:

### 2.1 The 22 Newly Added Monographs
1. **Hamzah ibn Abdul-Muttalib (`hamzah`):** *Sayyid ash-Shuhada* (Leader of the Martyrs), hero of Badr and Uhud.
2. **Ja'far ibn Abi Talib (`jafar`):** *Dhu al-Janahayn*, eloquent spokesman before the Negus of Abyssinia, martyr of Mu'tah.
3. **Ubayy ibn Ka'b (`ubayy-ibn-kab`):** Chief reciter of the Quran (Sayyid al-Qurra), master scribe of the revelation.
4. **Sa'd ibn Mu'adh (`sad-ibn-muadh`):** Leader of the Aws tribe whose passing caused the Throne of the Most Merciful to shake.
5. **Abu Ayyub al-Ansari (`abu-ayyub`):** The noble host of the Prophet ﷺ upon arrival in Madinah, buried beneath the walls of Constantinople.
6. **Amr ibn al-Aas (`amr-ibn-al-aas`):** Statesman, military strategist, and liberator of Egypt.
7. **Ammar ibn Yasir (`ammar-ibn-yasir`):** Early convert, son of the first martyrs in Islam (Sumayyah and Yasir).
8. **Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman (`hudhayfah`):** The trusted keeper of the Prophet's ﷺ confidential intelligence (*Sahib Sirr Rasulillah*).
9. **Abdullah ibn Umar (`ibn-umar`):** Jurist of Madinah, exemplary in strict emulation of the Prophetic Sunnah.
10. **Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (`abu-musa`):** Melodious reciter of the Quran, governor of Basra and Kufa.
11. **Zayd ibn Harithah (`zayd-ibn-harithah`):** The beloved adopted son of the Prophet ﷺ, the only companion explicitly named in the Holy Quran (Surah Al-Ahzab).
12. **Usamah ibn Zayd (`usamah-ibn-zayd`):** Young commander appointed by the Prophet ﷺ to lead the northern expedition.
13. **Sa'd ibn Ubadah (`sad-ibn-ubadah`):** Chieftain of the Khazraj, renowned for his extraordinary generosity and support of the Muhajirun.
14. **Abu ad-Darda (`abu-darda`):** Master of wisdom, ascetic jurist, and the first Qadi of Damascus.
15. **Jabir ibn Abdullah (`jabir-ibn-abdullah`):** Prolific Hadith narrator, devoted scholar who held educational halaqat in the Prophet's Mosque.
16. **Anas ibn Malik (`anas-ibn-malik`):** The personal attendant to the Prophet ﷺ for ten consecutive years in Madinah.
17. **Sawdah bint Zam'ah (`sawdah-bint-zamah`):** Mother of the Believers, early Muhajirah to Abyssinia, generous matriarch.
18. **Zaynab bint Khuzaymah (`zaynab-bint-khuzaymah`):** Mother of the Believers, famed as *Umm al-Masakin* (Mother of the Poor) for her boundless charity.
19. **Umm Ayman Barakah (`umm-ayman`):** The Prophet's ﷺ devoted maternal caregiver who accompanied him from childhood to adulthood.
20. **Rufaydah al-Aslamiyyah (`rufaydah`):** The first Muslim nurse, pioneer of mobile field hospitals during the Battle of the Trench and Khaybar.
21. **Al-Bara ibn Malik (`al-bara-ibn-malik`):** Fearless warrior whose prayers were answered, hero of the Garden of Death at Yamamah.
22. **Thabit ibn Qays (`thabit-ibn-qays`):** The eloquent orator (Khatib) of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and of the Ansar.

### 2.2 Scholarly Provenance & Citations
Every biography draws directly from:
- *Usd al-Ghabah fi Ma'rifat al-Sahabah* by Ibn al-Athir
- *Al-Isabah fi Tamyiz al-Sahabah* by Ibn Hajar al-Asqalani
- *Siyar A'lam al-Nubala* by Imam adh-Dhahabi
- *Al-Isti'ab fi Ma'rifat al-Ashab* by Ibn Abd al-Barr
