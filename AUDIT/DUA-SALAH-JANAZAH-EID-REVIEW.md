## 2. Salah & Purification Module Verification

### 2.1 The Five Daily Prayers (`salah-guide.html`)
- **Rak'ah Composition:** Authenticated across Fajr (2 Fard), Dhuhr (4 Sunnah, 4 Fard, 2 Sunnah, 2 Nafl), Asr (4 Sunnah ghair-mu'akkadah, 4 Fard), Maghrib (3 Fard, 2 Sunnah, 2 Nafl), and Isha (4 Sunnah, 4 Fard, 2 Sunnah, 2 Nafl, 3 Witr Wajib, 2 Nafl).
- **Core Recitations:**
  - *Thana (Opening Supplication):* `سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ...` (Abu Dawud #776, Tirmidhi #243).
  - *Ruku' Dhikr:* `سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ` (3x) (Sahih Muslim #772).
  - *Rising from Ruku':* `سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ • رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ` (Sahih al-Bukhari #795).
  - *Sujud Dhikr:* `سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَىٰ` (3x) (Sahih Muslim #772).
  - *Tashahhud:* Text of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud: `التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ...` (Sahih al-Bukhari #831, Sahih Muslim #402).
  - *Durood Ibrahim:* Complete dual benediction (Sahih al-Bukhari #3370).

### 2.2 Wudu, Ghusl & Tayammum (`wudu-guide.html`)
- **Four Quranic Fara'id:** Washing face, washing arms including elbows, wiping over head (Masah), and washing feet including ankles (*Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6*).
- **Sunan of Wudu:** Starting with Bismillah, washing hands to wrists 3 times, rinsing mouth (Madmadah), sniffing water into nose (Istinshaq), using Miswak, washing each limb 3 times, passing fingers through beard (Takhlil), and wiping ears.
- **Tayammum:** Validated upon absence of water or inability to use it; wiping face and hands once with clean earth (*Surah An-Nisa 4:43*).

## 3. Salat al-Janazah (Funeral Prayer) Deep Audit (`janazah-guide.html`)

### 3.1 Status & Essential Structure
- **Juridical Classification:** *Fard Kifayah* (communal obligation).
- **Peculiarity:** Entirely performed standing (*Qiyam*). Contains **zero Ruku'** (bowing) and **zero Sujud** (prostration).
- **Imam Standing Position:**
  - Deceased Male: At the head or upper chest (*Sunan Abi Dawud #3194*).
  - Deceased Female: At the waist / midpoint (*Sunan Abi Dawud #3194*).

### 3.2 The Four Takbirs Step-by-Step
1. **Takbir 1:** Opening Takbir + Thana / Surah Al-Fatihah.
   - *Hanafi School:* Thana with funeral addition (`سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ... وَجَلَّ ثَنَاؤُكَ`); Surah Al-Fatihah is permissible with the intention of Dua.
   - *Shafi'i & Hanbali Schools:* Surah Al-Fatihah is an obligatory pillar (*Rukn*) based on the general Hadith: *"There is no prayer for the one who does not recite the Opening of the Book"* (*Sahih al-Bukhari #756*).
2. **Takbir 2:** Durood Ibrahim on Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (*Sahih al-Bukhari #3370*).
3. **Takbir 3:** Specific Supplications for the Deceased:
   - **For Adult (Male/Female):**
     > `اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِحَيِّنَا وَمَيِّتِنَا، وَشَاهِدِنَا وَغَائِبِنَا، وَصَغِيرِنَا وَكَبِيرِنَا، وَذَكَرِنَا وَأُنْثَانَا. اللَّهُمَّ مَنْ أَحْيَيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَأَحْيِهِ عَلَى الْإِسْلَامِ، وَمَنْ تَوَفَّيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَتَوَفَّهُ عَلَى الْإِيمَانِ. اللَّهُمَّ لَا تَحْرِمْنَا أَجْرَهُ، وَلَا تُضِلَّنَا بَعْدَهُ.`  
     > *(Sunan Abi Dawud #3201, Jami` at-Tirmidhi #1024 — Sahih)*
   - **For a Deceased Pre-Pubescent Child:**
     > `اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ لَنَا فَرَطًا، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا أَجْرًا وَذُخْرًا، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا شَافِعًا وَمُشَفَّعًا.`  
     > *(Sahih al-Bukhari ta'liqan, Al-Bayhaqi in As-Sunan al-Kubra 4/9)*
4. **Takbir 4 & Tasleem:**
   - *Hanafi School:* Two Tasleems (turning face to right, then left).
   - *Shafi'i, Maliki & Hanbali Schools:* One Tasleem to the right is the Sunnah standard.

## 4. Salat al-Eid (Festival Prayers) Deep Audit (`eid-guide.html`)

### 4.1 Comparison of Additional Takbirs
The portal accurately explains the two major methodologies without dogmatism:

| Method | Schools | 1st Rak'ah Extra Takbirs | 2nd Rak'ah Extra Takbirs | Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **6 Extra Takbirs** | **Hanafi** | **3** after Thana before Fatihah | **3** after Surah recitation before Ruku' | Practice of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (*Musannaf Abdur-Razzaq #5687*) |
| **12 Extra Takbirs** | **Shafi'i & Hanbali** | **7** after opening Takbir | **5** upon rising from prostration before Fatihah | Hadith of Abdullah ibn Amr (*Sunan Abi Dawud #1151, Tirmidhi #536*) |

### 4.2 Takbirat al-Tashreeq
- **Arabic Text:** `اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ`
- **Timing:** Commences from the **Fajr prayer of the 9th of Dhul Hijjah** (Day of Arafah) until the **Asr prayer of the 13th of Dhul Hijjah** (totaling 23 consecutive obligatory prayers).
- **Ruling:** *Wajib* / emphasized Sunnah immediately after every Fard prayer.

### 4.3 Zakat al-Fitr & Qurbani (Udhiyah)
- **Zakat al-Fitr:** Obligatory before the Eid al-Fitr prayer starts (*Sahih al-Bukhari #1503*). Amount: 1 Sa' (~2.5 to 3.0 kg) of staple grain or equivalent local value.
- **Qurbani:** Sacrificed during the 3 days of Tashreeq (10th, 11th, 12th Dhul Hijjah). Age criteria: Sheep (1 yr), Cow (2 yrs), Camel (5 yrs). Distribution recommended into 3 parts (family, neighbors, needy).
