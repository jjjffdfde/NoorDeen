Step-by-Step Wudu (Ablution) Guide

"O you who have believed, when you rise to prayer, wash your faces and your forearms to the elbows..." (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6)

The 4 Obligatory Acts (Fara'id) of Wudu:

  1. Washing the entire face from hairline to bottom of the chin, and earlobe to earlobe.
  2. Washing both arms from fingertips up to and including the elbows.
  3. Wiping (Masah) over the head.
  4. Washing both feet including the ankles.

1. Intention (Niyyah) & Saying Bismillah

Form the sincere intention in the heart to purify yourself for Allah's worship, and say:

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Bismillaah (In the name of Allah).

2. Washing the Hands to Wrists (3 times)

Wash both hands thoroughly up to the wrists three times, ensuring water passes between all fingers.

3. Rinsing the Mouth (Madmadah) (3 times)

Take a handful of water with the right hand, swish it thoroughly inside the mouth, and spit it out. Repeat thrice.

4. Sniffing Water into the Nostrils (Istinshaq) (3 times)

Sniff a small amount of water into the nose using the right hand and blow it out using the left hand. Repeat thrice.

5. Washing the Face (3 times) [FARD]

Wash the entire face three times, ensuring no dry spots remain between hairline and chin, or ear to ear.

6. Washing Forearms to the Elbows (3 times) [FARD]

Wash the right arm from fingertips past the elbow 3 times, then repeat the same for the left arm 3 times.

7. Wiping Head and Ears (Masah) [FARD]

Wet hands and wipe over the top of the head from front to back and return, then wipe the inside and back of ears.

8. Washing the Feet to the Ankles (3 times) [FARD]

Wash the right foot including the heel and ankle bone 3 times, rubbing between toes. Repeat for left foot.

Supplication Upon Completing Wudu

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said that whoever recites this after wudu, all eight gates of Paradise are opened for them (Sahih Muslim #234):

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ. اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْنِي مِنَ التَّوَّابِينَ وَاجْعَلْنِي مِنَ الْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ.

Ash-hadu al-laa ilaaha illal-laahu wahdahoo laa shareeka lahoo, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhoo wa Rasooluh. Allaahummaj-'alnee minat-tawwaabeena waj-'alnee minal-mutatahhireen.

"I bear witness that there is no deity except Allah alone without partner, and Muhammad is His servant and Messenger. O Allah! Make me among those who constantly repent and among those who purify themselves."

Acts That Invalidate Wudu (Nawaqid al-Wudu)

  • Any bodily discharge from the private passages (urine, stool, flatulence).
  • Deep sleep during which awareness and muscular control are lost.
  • Loss of consciousness, fainting, or anesthesia.
  • Direct skin-to-skin touch of private parts with lust (according to standard juristic rulings).
