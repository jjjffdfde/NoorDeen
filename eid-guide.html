صَلَاةُ الْعِيدَيْنِ — EID AL-FITR & EID AL-ADHA

Complete Step-by-Step Eid Prayer Guide

The two Eid prayers celebrate the completion of Ramadan (Eid al-Fitr) and the sacred rites of Hajj and sacrifice (Eid al-Adha). The prayer consists of two Rak'ahs in congregation with extra Takbirs, followed by the sermon (Khutbah).

Essential Rules & Nature of Eid Prayer

  • Ruling: Wajib (obligatory) upon adult males in the Hanafi school; Sunnah Mu'akkadah (strongly emphasized Sunnah) in the Maliki and Shafi'i schools; Fard Kifayah in the Hanbali school.
  • Timing: Begins approximately 15–20 minutes after sunrise (once the sun rises the height of a spear / Ishraq) until shortly before the solar zenith (Zawal).
  • No Adhan & No Iqamah: Jabir ibn Samurah narrated: "I prayed with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ on Eid not once or twice without an Adhan or Iqamah." (Sahih Muslim #887).
  • Khutbah Comes After: Unlike Friday Jumu'ah where the Khutbah precedes prayer, on Eid the two Rak'ahs are prayed first, followed by the Khutbah (Sahih al-Bukhari #957). Listening to the sermon is a Sunnah.
METHOD 1 (HANAFI MADHHAB) طَرِيقَةُ السَّادَةِ الْحَنَفِيَّةِ (٦ تَكْبِيرَاتٍ زَائِدَةٍ)

The 6 Extra Takbirs Method

Followed widely across South Asia, Turkey, Central Asia, and the Balkans according to the narrations of Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (may Allah be pleased with him):

First Rak'ah (3 Extra Takbirs after Thana):

  1. Make the intention (Niyyah) in your heart: "I intend to perform 2 Rak'ahs of Wajib Eid prayer with 6 extra Takbirs behind this Imam."
  2. Imam says opening "Allahu Akbar" (Takbirat al-Ihram). Raise hands to earlobes, fold hands below the navel, and recite Thana (Subhanakallahumma...) silently.
  3. 1st Extra Takbir: Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Raise hands to earlobes, then release them down at your sides.
  4. 2nd Extra Takbir: Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Raise hands, then release them down at your sides.
  5. 3rd Extra Takbir: Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Raise hands, and this time fold hands below the navel.
  6. Imam recites Ta'awwudh, Tasmiyah, Surah Al-Fatihah, and a Surah (often Surah Al-A'la, 87) aloud. Listen attentively.
  7. Perform standard Ruku' and two prostrations (Sujud) as in normal Salah, then stand for the 2nd Rak'ah.

Second Rak'ah (3 Extra Takbirs before Ruku'):

  1. Stand up and immediately keep hands folded below the navel.
  2. Imam recites Surah Al-Fatihah and a Surah (often Surah Al-Ghashiyah, 88) aloud.
  3. 1st Extra Takbir: After reciting the Surah, Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Raise hands to ears, then release them to your sides.
  4. 2nd Extra Takbir: Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Raise hands, then release them to your sides.
  5. 3rd Extra Takbir: Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Raise hands, then release them to your sides.
  6. 4th Takbir (Bowing): Imam says "Allahu Akbar" without raising hands and bows directly into Ruku'. Follow into Ruku'.
  7. Complete the two prostrations, sit for Tashahhud (At-Tahiyyat), recite Durood Ibrahim and Dua, and complete the prayer with Tasleem to the right and left.
METHOD 2 (SHAFI'I & HANBALI) طَرِيقَةُ الشَّافِعِيَّةِ وَالْحَنَابِلَةِ (١٢ تَكْبِيرَةً)

The 12 Extra Takbirs Method (7 in 1st Rak'ah, 5 in 2nd)

Followed widely in the Middle East, North Africa, Egypt, East Africa, Indonesia, and Malaysia according to the narration of Abdullah ibn Amr (may Allah be pleased with him): "The Prophet ﷺ made seven takbirs in the first rak'ah and five in the second during the Eid prayer" (Sunan Abi Dawud #1151, Sunan at-Tirmidhi #536):

First Rak'ah (7 Extra Takbirs before recitation):

  • Opening Takbir (Takbirat al-Ihram) + opening supplication (Du'a al-Istiftah).
  • Imam says 7 consecutive Takbirs, raising hands with each.
  • Between the Takbirs, it is Sunnah to softly praise Allah:
    سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، وَلَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
    Subhan Allāh, wal-hamdu lillāh, wa laa ilaaha ill-Allāhu, wallāhu akbar.
  • Imam seeks refuge, recites Surah Al-Fatihah, and Surah Qaf (50) or Al-A'la (87).
  • Complete Ruku' and two prostrations.

Second Rak'ah (5 Extra Takbirs before recitation):

  • Stand up from the second prostration with the standard Takbir of rising.
  • Imam says 5 consecutive Takbirs before reciting, raising hands with each.
  • Recite Dhikr between them: Subhan Allāh, wal-hamdu lillāh, wa laa ilaaha ill-Allāhu, wallāhu akbar.
  • Imam recites Surah Al-Fatihah and Surah Al-Qamar (54) or Al-Ghashiyah (88).
  • Complete Ruku', Sujud, Tashahhud, Durood, and Tasleem.
EID AL-ADHA ESSENTIAL تَكْبِيرَاتُ التَّشْرِيقِ

Takbirat al-Tashreeq (Words & Timings)

It is Wajib / emphasized Sunnah upon every adult Muslim (men and women, in congregation or individually) to recite the Takbir aloud immediately following every obligatory (Fard) prayer:

اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، وَلِلَّهِ الْحَمْدُ.

Allāhu Akbar, Allāhu Akbar, Laa ilaaha ill-Allāhu, wallāhu Akbar, Allāhu Akbar, wa lillāhil-hamd.

Meaning: Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest; there is no deity worthy of worship besides Allah, and Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, and to Allah belongs all praise.

اللہ سب سے بڑا ہے، اللہ سب سے بڑا ہے، اللہ کے سوا کوئی معبود نہیں، اور اللہ سب سے بڑا ہے، اللہ سب سے بڑا ہے، اور تمام تر تعریفیں اللہ ہی کے لیے ہیں۔

Exact Days & Timings (23 Fard Prayers):

• Starts: Fajr prayer of the 9th of Dhul Hijjah (Day of Arafah).
• Ends: Asr prayer of the 13th of Dhul Hijjah (the final day of Tashreeq).
• Total: Recited after 23 consecutive obligatory prayers.

Sunan & Etiquettes of the Day of Eid

1. Ghusl & Clean Clothes:

Performing full ritual bath (Ghusl) on the morning of Eid, applying pleasant non-alcoholic perfume (for men), and dressing in one's finest, clean modest attire.

2. Eating Sunnah (Fitr vs Adha):

• Eid al-Fitr: Eat an odd number of dates before setting out (Sahih al-Bukhari #953).
• Eid al-Adha: Refrain from eating until returning from prayer and eat from your sacrificial meat (Sunan at-Tirmidhi #542).

3. Walking via Different Paths:

Taking one route when going to the prayer area and returning home by a different path to increase footsteps of goodness and greet more Muslims (Sahih al-Bukhari #986).

4. Sunnah Eid Greeting:
تَقَبَّلَ اللَّهُ مِنَّا وَمِنْكُم

Taqabbal Allāhu minnā wa minkum — "May Allah accept [good deeds] from us and from you." (Sahaba tradition, Fath al-Bari 2/446).

Zakat al-Fitr & Qurbani (Udhiyah) Overview

🌾 Zakat al-Fitr (Eid al-Fitr)

  • Obligatory: On every Muslim (head of household pays for dependents) who has sustenance exceeding one day and night.
  • Timing: Must be distributed before the Eid prayer starts. If given after, it counts as ordinary charity.
  • Amount: 1 Sa' (~2.5 to 3.0 kg) of staple food grain (wheat, barley, dates, rice) or equivalent local monetary value.

🐑 Qurbani / Udhiyah (Eid al-Adha)

  • Eligibility: Wajib upon Muslims possessing Nisab threshold of wealth on the days of sacrifice (10th, 11th, 12th Dhul Hijjah).
  • Animals: Goat/sheep (1 person), cow/camel (up to 7 shares). Animals must be healthy and meet age minimums.
  • Distribution: Recommended into three parts: 1/3 for household, 1/3 for relatives/friends, 1/3 for needy families.
