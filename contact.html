Editorial Integrity & Review Protocol

The Islamic Knowledge Portal is committed to the highest standards of Islamic scholarship (E-E-A-T). Every report regarding text integrity is cross-checked against:

  • Quranic Text: King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex standard (Hafs 'an 'Asim, 1405 AH edition).
  • Hadith Grading: Verification through Darussalam and Al-Maktaba Al-Shamela reference standards.
  • Translations: Verified public-domain or authorized scholarly works (Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry for Urdu, Saheeh International for English).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you verify reported errors?

When a reader reports an ayah or hadith reference, two independent researchers cross-reference the report against our physical reference prints and authoritative online scholarly repositories before any change is published.

Can I contribute new language translations?

Yes. We welcome submissions from accredited Islamic translation institutions and qualified native-speaking scholars. Please choose "Translation Quality" in the contact form.

Is this website affiliated with a specific political party?

No. The Islamic Knowledge Portal is strictly an educational, non-partisan, non-sectarian public platform dedicated to classical mainstream Islamic teachings based on the Quran and authentic Prophetic Sunnah.
