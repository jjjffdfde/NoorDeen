Sahih al-Bukhari #1 [Revelation]
Sahih (Authentic) Iman
إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى.
"Actions are according to intentions, and every person will have only that which he intended."
تمام اعمال کا دارومدار نیتوں پر ہے اور ہر انسان کے لیے وہی ہے جس کی اس نے نیت کی۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Centrality of Sincere Intention

Imam ash-Shafi‘i and Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal declared that this hadith encompasses one-third of all Islamic knowledge. Human accountability centers upon intention (Niyyah). Practically, even ordinary habits such as eating, sleeping, or working are transformed into rewarded acts of worship when undertaken with the intention of gaining strength to worship Allah and support one's family.

Narrated by: Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)
Sahih al-Bukhari #13 [Belief]
Sahih (Authentic) Akhlaq
لاَ يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ.
"None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother that which he loves for himself."
تم میں سے کوئی شخص اس وقت تک سچا مومن نہیں ہو سکتا جب تک کہ وہ اپنے بھائی کے لیے وہی پسند نہ کرے جو اپنے لیے پسند کرتا ہے۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: Altruism as the Criterion of Complete Faith

Scholars explain that the negation of faith ("none of you truly believes") signifies the negation of spiritual perfection, not the complete departure from Islam. A believer's heart must be purified of malice, jealousy (Hasad), and selfish rivalry, genuinely rejoicing when good fortune reaches other human beings.

Narrated by: Anas ibn Malik (RA)
Sahih Muslim #2564 [Virtue & Character]
Sahih (Authentic) Iman
إِنَّ اللَّهَ لاَ يَنْظُرُ إِلَى صُوَرِكُمْ وَأَمْوَالِكُمْ وَلَكِنْ يَنْظُرُ إِلَى قُلُوبِكُمْ وَأَعْمَالِكُمْ.
"Verily, Allah does not look at your outward appearance or your wealth, but He looks at your hearts and your deeds."
بے شک اللہ تعالیٰ تمہاری صورتوں اور تمہارے مالوں کی طرف نہیں دیکھتا، بلکہ وہ تمہارے دلوں اور تمہارے اعمال کی طرف دیکھتا ہے۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Sanctity of the Spiritual Heart (Qalb)

Worldly standards judge humans by superficial aesthetics, ethnic lineage, and material net worth. Allah the Almighty looks exclusively upon the spiritual purity of the inward heart and the righteousness of outward conduct. An outwardly humble deed animated by pure sincerity weighs heavier in the Scales than oceans of ostentatious charity.

Narrated by: Abu Hurairah (RA)
Sahih Muslim #223 [Purification]
Sahih (Authentic) Salah
الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ تَمْلأُ الْمِيزَانَ، وَالصَّلاَةُ نُورٌ، وَالصَّدَقَةُ بُرْهَانٌ.
"Purification is half of faith, and 'Alhamdulillah' fills the scale, prayer is a light, and charity is proof."
پاکیزگی نصف ایمان ہے، اور الحمد للہ ترازو کو بھر دیتا ہے، نماز نور ہے اور صدقہ دلیل ہے۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Dual Dimensions of Purification

Purification is termed half of faith because spiritual salvation comprises two fundamental movements: Takhliyah (cleansing the heart of sins, pride, and false deities) and Tahliyah (adorning it with Tawheed and good deeds). Physical Wudu and Ghusl externally manifest this inner spiritual purification.

Narrated by: Abu Malik al-Ash'ari (RA)
Jami` at-Tirmidhi #1924 [Righteousness]
Sahih (Al-Albani) Mercy
الرَّاحِمُونَ يَرْحَمُهُمُ الرَّحْمَنُ، ارْحَمُوا مَنْ فِي الأَرْضِ يَرْحَمْكُمْ مَنْ فِي السَّمَاءِ.
"The merciful will be shown mercy by the Most Merciful. Be merciful to those on earth, and the One in the heavens will show mercy to you."
رحم کرنے والوں پر رحمن رحم فرماتا ہے۔ تم زمین والوں پر رحم کرو، آسمان والا تم پر رحم فرمائے گا۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: Divine Reciprocity in Mercy

Known in classical Hadith sciences as Hadith al-Musalsal bil-Awwaliyyah (traditionally the first hadith a student receives from their teacher). It establishes universal benevolence: whoever seeks the expansive mercy of the Creator must demonstrate active tenderness, justice, and mercy toward all creations on earth, including animals.

Narrated by: Abdullah ibn Amr (RA)
Sunan Abi Dawud #4799 [General Behavior (Adab)]
Sahih (Al-Albani) Akhlaq
مَنْ لَمْ يَشْكُرِ النَّاسَ لَمْ يَشْكُرِ اللَّهَ.
"Whoever does not thank people does not thank Allah."
جو لوگوں کا شکر ادا نہیں کرتا، وہ اللہ کا بھی شکر ادا نہیں کرتا۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: Moral Excellence on the Divine Scales

Good character (Husn al-Khuluq)—characterized by a cheerful countenance, withholding harm, and abundant generosity—outweighs voluminous voluntary worship that lacks moral rectitude. The Prophet ﷺ declared that the closest people to him on Judgment Day will be those with the finest character.

Narrated by: Abu Hurairah (RA)
Sunan an-Nasa'i #3140 [Jihad]
Hasan (Al-Albani) Akhlaq
الْجَنَّةُ تَحْتَ أَقْدَامِ الأُمَّهَاتِ.
"Paradise lies beneath the feet of mothers."
جنت ماؤں کے قدموں تلے ہے۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: Devotion to Parents as the Path to Paradise

When Jahima (RA) sought to join the military expedition, the Prophet ﷺ asked if his mother was alive. Upon learning she was, he commanded him: "Stay with her, for Paradise lies beneath her feet." Serving an elderly mother, lowering one's wing of humility, and seeking her blessing takes precedence over voluntary expeditions.

Narrated by: Mu'awiyah ibn Jahima (RA)
Sahih al-Bukhari #5027 [Virtues of Quran]
Sahih (Authentic) Knowledge
خَيْرُكُمْ مَنْ تَعَلَّمَ الْقُرْآنَ وَعَلَّمَهُ.
"The best among you are those who learn the Quran and teach it to others."
تم میں سب سے بہترین شخص وہ ہے جو قرآن مجید سیکھے اور اسے دوسروں کو سکھائے۔
Narrated by: Uthman ibn Affan (RA)
Sahih al-Bukhari #8 [Faith (Kitab al-Iman)]
Sahih (Authentic) • Muttafaqun 'Alayh (Bukhari & Muslim) Iman
بُنِيَ الإِسْلاَمُ عَلَى خَمْسٍ: شَهَادَةِ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ، وَإِقَامِ الصَّلاَةِ، وَإِيتَاءِ الزَّكَاةِ، وَالْحَجِّ، وَصَوْمِ رَمَضَانَ.
"Islam is built upon five pillars: testifying that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, establishing regular prayer, paying Zakat, the Hajj pilgrimage, and fasting Ramadan."
اسلام کی بنیاد پانچ چیزوں پر رکھی گئی ہے: اس بات کی گواہی دینا کہ اللہ کے سوا کوئی معبود نہیں اور محمد ﷺ اللہ کے رسول ہیں، نماز قائم کرنا، زکوٰۃ ادا کرنا، حج کرنا اور رمضان کے روزے رکھنا۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Architectural Metaphor of Islam

Ibn Umar (RA) narrated this fundamental tradition comparing Islam to a majestic canopy sustained by five load-bearing pillars. The Shahadah is the foundational bedrock, Salah is the central tent pole, and Zakat, Sawm, and Hajj constitute the outer pillars. If the central pillar collapses, the tent cannot remain upright.

Narrated by: Abdullah ibn Umar (RA)
Sahih Muslim #137 [Prayer (Kitab as-Salah)]
Sahih (Authentic) • Imam Muslim & Tirmidhi Salah
إِنَّ أَوَّلَ مَا يُحَاسَبُ بِهِ الْعَبْدُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ مِنْ عَمَلِهِ صَلاَتُهُ، فَإِنْ صَلُحَتْ فَقَدْ أَفْلَحَ وَأَنْجَحَ، وَإِنْ فَسَدَتْ فَقَدْ خَابَ وَخَسِرَ.
"The first matter that the slave will be brought to account for on the Day of Judgment is his prayer. If it is sound, then he has triumphed and succeeded; but if it is corrupt, he has failed and lost."
قیامت کے دن بندے کے اعمال میں سب سے پہلے جس چیز کا حساب لیا جائے گا وہ نماز ہے۔ اگر وہ درست نکلی تو وہ فلاح پا گیا اور کامیاب ہو گیا، اور اگر وہ خراب نکلی تو وہ نامراد اور خسارے میں رہا۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Supreme Priority of the Five Daily Prayers

Salah is the very first accountability point on the Day of Judgment. The Prophet ﷺ clarified that if one's obligatory prayers are complete and sound, all other deeds will be facilitated. If obligatory prayers have deficiencies, Allah in His boundless mercy commands the angels to see if the slave has voluntary (Nafl) prayers to compensate for the shortcomings.

Narrated by: Abu Hurairah (RA)
Sahih al-Bukhari #1904 [Fasting (Kitab as-Sawm)]
Sahih (Authentic) • Muttafaqun 'Alayh (Bukhari & Muslim) Fasting
الصِّيَامُ جُنَّةٌ، فَلَا يَرْفُثْ وَلَا يَجْهَلْ، وَإِنِ امْرُؤٌ قَاتَلَهُ أَوْ شَاتَمَهُ فَلْيَقُلْ: إِنِّي صَائِمٌ - مَرَّتَيْنِ. وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ لَخُلُوفُ فَمِ الصَّائِمِ أَطْيَبُ عِنْدَ اللَّهِ تَعَالَى مِنْ رِيحِ الْمِسْكِ.
"Fasting is a protective shield. So when one of you is fasting, he should neither indulge in obscenity nor act foolishly. If anyone fights him or insults him, let him say twice: 'I am fasting.' By Him in Whose Hand is my soul, the breath of a fasting person is sweeter to Allah than the scent of musk."
روزہ ایک ڈھال ہے۔ پس جب تم میں سے کوئی روزے سے ہو تو نہ بے ہودہ گفتگو کرے اور نہ جہالت والی حرکت۔ اگر کوئی اس سے لڑے یا گالی دے تو وہ دو مرتبہ کہے: 'میں روزے سے ہوں۔' اس ذات کی قسم جس کے ہاتھ میں میری جان ہے، روزہ دار کے منہ کی بو اللہ کے نزدیک کستوری کی خوشبو سے بھی زیادہ پاکیزہ ہے۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Spiritual Shield and Musk of Fasting

Fasting acts as a spiritual fortress guarding the believer against sins, lust, and hellfire. The fasting person is trained in supreme self-restraint (Sabr). Responding to hostility with the calm affirmation "I am fasting" neutralizes provocation and preserves spiritual tranquility.

Narrated by: Abu Hurairah (RA)
Sahih al-Bukhari #1395 [Obligatory Charity (Kitab az-Zakat)]
Sahih (Authentic) • Sahih Muslim 2588 Zakat
مَا نَقَصَتْ صَدَقَةٌ مِنْ مَالٍ، وَمَا زَادَ اللَّهُ عَبْدًا بِعَفْوٍ إِلاَّ عِزًّا، وَمَا تَوَاضَعَ أَحَدٌ لِلَّهِ إِلاَّ رَفَعَهُ اللَّهُ.
"Charity does not decrease wealth. No one forgives another except that Allah increases him in honor, and no one humbles himself for the sake of Allah except that Allah elevates his status."
صدقہ کسی مال میں کمی نہیں کرتا، اور معاف کر دینے سے اللہ بندے کی عزت میں اضافہ ہی فرماتا ہے، اور جو شخص بھی اللہ کے لیے عاجزی اختیار کرتا ہے اللہ اسے بلندی عطا فرماتا ہے۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: Divine Multiplication of Sincere Charity

While mathematical subtraction occurs when giving money, spiritual reality increases wealth through Barakah (divine blessing), protection from calamities, and eternal compensation in the Akhirah. Charity purifies accumulated wealth and extinguishes sins like water extinguishes fire.

Narrated by: Abu Hurairah (RA)
Sahih al-Bukhari #1773 [Umrah & Hajj]
Sahih (Authentic) • Muttafaqun 'Alayh (Bukhari & Muslim) Hajj
الْعُمْرَةُ إِلَى الْعُمْرَةِ كَفَّارَةٌ لِمَا بَيْنَهُمَا، وَالْحَجُّ الْمَبْرُورُ لَيْسَ لَهُ جَزَاءٌ إِلَّا الْجَنَّةُ.
"An Umrah to another Umrah is an expiation for what sins occurred between them, and an accepted, upright Hajj (Hajj Mabrur) has no reward except Paradise."
ایک عمرہ دوسرے عمرے تک کے درمیانی گناہوں کا کفارہ ہے، اور حج مبرور کا بدلہ جنت کے سوا کچھ نہیں۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Pure State of Hajj Mabrur

An accepted, upright Hajj—free of obscenity, dispute, and unlawful earnings—completely cleanses the pilgrim from sins, returning them pure like the day their mother gave birth to them. Its ultimate reward is nothing less than the eternal gardens of Paradise.

Narrated by: Abu Hurairah (RA)
Sunan Abi Dawud #1479 [Prayer (Witr & Dua)]
Sahih (Al-Albani) • Abu Dawud & Tirmidhi Dua
الدُّعَاءُ هُوَ الْعِبَادَةُ، ثُمَّ قَرَأَ: {وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ادْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ}.
"Supplication is worship itself. Then the Prophet ﷺ recited: 'And your Lord says: Call upon Me; I will respond to you' (Surah Ghafir 40:60)."
دعا ہی اصل عبادت ہے۔ پھر آپ ﷺ نے یہ آیت تلاوت فرمائی: 'اور تمہارے پروردگار نے فرمایا مجھ سے دعا کرو میں تمہاری دعا قبول کروں گا۔'
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: Supplication as the Soul of Worship

Dua is described as the essence and core of Ibadah because it embodies utter humility, complete acknowledgment of human helplessness, and total reliance upon Allah's infinite power and boundless generosity.

Narrated by: An-Nu'man ibn Bashir (RA)
Jami` at-Tirmidhi #2516 [Day of Judgment (Kitab Sifat al-Qiyamah)]
Sahih (Al-Albani) • At-Tirmidhi / Al-Albani Iman
يَا غُلاَمُ إِنِّي أُعَلِّمُكَ كَلِمَاتٍ: احْفَظِ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ، احْفَظِ اللَّهَ تَجِدْهُ تُجَاهَكَ، إِذَا سَأَلْتَ فَاسْأَلِ اللَّهَ، وَإِذَا اسْتَعَنْتَ فَاسْتَعِنْ بِاللَّهِ.
"O young man, I will teach you some words: Be mindful of Allah, and Allah will protect you. Be mindful of Allah, and you will find Him before you. If you ask, ask Allah; and if you seek assistance, seek help from Allah."
اے لڑکے! میں تمہیں چند باتیں سکھاتا ہوں: تم اللہ کے احکام کی حفاظت کرو اللہ تمہاری حفاظت کرے گا، تم اللہ کو یاد رکھو اسے اپنے سامنے پاؤ گے، جب مانگنا ہو تو اللہ سے مانگو اور جب مدد طلب کرنی ہو تو اللہ سے مدد چاہو۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Comprehensive Advice to Ibn Abbas

This master prophetic testament lays down the foundations of Tawheed, absolute reliance (Tawakkul), and faith in divine decree (Qadr). Whatever was destined to reach you could never have missed you, and whatever missed you was never destined to reach you.

Narrated by: Abdullah ibn Abbas (RA)
Sunan an-Nasa'i #5431 [Seeking Refuge (Kitab al-Isti'adhah)]
Sahih (Al-Albani) • An-Nasa'i / Ibn Majah Knowledge
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا، وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا.
"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, wholesome pure provision, and deeds that are accepted."
اے اللہ! میں تجھ سے نفع بخش علم، پاکیزہ حلال رزق اور قبول ہونے والے عمل کا سوال کرتا ہوں۔
Narrated by: Umm Salamah (RA)
Sunan Ibn Majah #224 [The Book of the Sunnah (Muqaddimah)]
Sahih (Al-Albani) • Ibn Majah / Al-Albani Knowledge
طَلَبُ الْعِلْمِ فَرِيضَةٌ عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ.
"Seeking knowledge is an absolute religious duty upon every single Muslim."
علم کا حاصل کرنا ہر مسلمان (مرد و عورت) پر فرض ہے۔
📖 Fiqh al-Hadith: The Obligation of Seeking Beneficial Knowledge

Imam al-Ghazali and classical jurists explain that seeking knowledge of the essentials of faith—how to pray, purify oneself, fast, calculate Zakat, conduct lawful transactions, and purify the heart from spiritual diseases—is Fard al-Ayn (an individual obligation upon every mature Muslim).

Narrated by: Anas ibn Malik (RA)

Sahih Muslim #70 [Faith (Kitab al-Iman)]
Sahih (Authentic) • Imam Muslim Iman
الإِيمَانُ بِضْعٌ وَسَبْعُونَ - أَوْ بِضْعٌ وَسِتُّونَ - شُعْبَةً، فَأَفْضَلُهَا قَوْلُ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ، وَأَدْنَاهَا إِمَاطَةُ الأَذَى عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ، وَالْحَيَاءُ شُعْبَةٌ مِنَ الإِيمَانِ.
"Faith has over seventy branches — the highest of which is the declaration that there is no god but Allah, and the lowest of which is removing a harmful obstacle from the road; and modesty is a branch of faith."
ایمان کی ستر سے کچھ اوپر شاخیں ہیں، جن میں سب سے افضل 'لا الہ الا اللہ' کا اقرار ہے اور ادنیٰ ترین راستے سے تکلیف دہ چیز کو ہٹانا ہے، اور حیا بھی ایمان کی ایک شاخ ہے۔
Narrated by: Abu Hurairah (RA)
Sunan Abi Dawud #4985 [Manners (Kitab al-Adab)]
Sahih (Authentic) • Muttafaqun 'Alayh (Bukhari & Muslim) Akhlaq
لَيْسَ الشَّدِيدُ بِالصُّرَعَةِ، إِنَّمَا الشَّدِيدُ الَّذِي يَمْلِكُ نَفْسَهُ عِنْدَ الْغَضَبِ.
"The strong person is not the one who overcomes others in wrestling; the strong person is the one who controls himself at the time of anger."
طاقتور وہ شخص نہیں جو دوسروں کو پچھاڑ دے، بلکہ اصل طاقتور وہ ہے جو غصے کے وقت اپنے نفس پر قابو رکھے۔
Narrated by: Abu Hurairah (RA)
Sunan Ibn Majah #4102 [Asceticism (Kitab az-Zuhd)]
Hasan (Al-Albani) • Ibn Majah / Al-Albani Akhlaq
ازْهَدْ فِي الدُّنْيَا يُحِبَّكَ اللَّهُ، وَازْهَدْ فِيمَا فِي أَيْدِي النَّاسِ يُحِبَّكَ النَّاسُ.
"Renounce worldly greed and attachment to materialism and Allah will love you; and renounce what is in the possession of people and people will love you."
دنیا سے بے رغبتی اختیار کرو اللہ تم سے محبت کرے گا، اور جو کچھ لوگوں کے پاس ہے اس سے بے نیازی برتو لوگ تم سے محبت کریں گے۔
Narrated by: Sahl ibn Sa'd al-Sa'idi (RA)
