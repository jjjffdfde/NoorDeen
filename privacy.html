Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Our commitment to transparency, privacy, and user dignity.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Islamic Knowledge Portal does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide your real name, email address, or phone number to read the Holy Quran, explore Hadith, or access daily supplications.

2. Local Device Storage (LocalStorage)

We utilize modern browser localStorage exclusively on your device to remember your personal preferences:

  • Language Selection: Stores your chosen interface language code (e.g., 'en', 'ar', 'ur').
  • Theme Mode: Stores your visual preference ('light' or 'dark').
  • Bookmarks: Stores verses or supplications you have intentionally marked to revisit.
  • Font Settings: Stores your preferred Quranic Arabic reading font size.

This data remains exclusively on your personal device and is never sent to our servers.

3. Cookies & Advertising Transparency

To ensure platform sustainability, this website is structured for potential future integration with Google AdSense. In the event advertising is activated:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (www.google.com/settings/ads).

We strictly forbid deceptive advertising, pop-unders, auto-playing video audio, or advertisements that disrupt religious reading.

4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to submit feedback, please reach out via our contact channels or open an inquiry on our public repository.
