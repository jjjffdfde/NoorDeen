LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION COMPLIANCE

Privacy & Cookie Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Explicit disclosures regarding Google AdSense, DoubleClick DART cookies, GDPR, CCPA, and your privacy rights.

1. Introduction & Overview

The Islamic Knowledge Portal ("we," "our," or "the platform") is dedicated to providing authentic Islamic educational resources with complete privacy integrity. We believe that spiritual study, reading the Holy Quran, and exploring Hadith should never require sacrificing personal privacy. We do not require registration, mandatory logins, or personal profiling to access our religious archives.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

To sustain server costs, bandwidth, and open-access publishing for Muslims globally, our website utilizes or may utilize Google AdSense to display advertisements. Please review Google's advertising policies and disclosures:

  • Third-Party Vendors: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on our website.
  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google’s use of the DART cookie enables it to serve personalized ads to users based on their visit to our site and other sites across the Internet.
  • Opting Out of DART Cookies: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page. Alternatively, users may opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • Zero Inappropriate Content Guarantee: In accordance with Islamic ethics, all ad categories related to gambling, alcohol, adult content, predatory lending, or unlawful products are strictly blocked on our publisher account.

3. Local Device Storage (LocalStorage)

We store essential user preferences entirely on your local device browser using HTML5 localStorage. This data never traverses our servers and includes:

  • islamic_portal_theme: Remembers light or dark mode preference.
  • islamic_portal_lang: Remembers your selected language (e.g. English, Urdu, Arabic).
  • islamic_portal_bookmarks: Saves verses you bookmark to review later.
  • islamic_portal_last_read: Remembers your reading position in the Quran.
  • islamic_portal_font_size: Remembers your Quranic Arabic font zoom level.

You can clear this data at any time through your browser's "Clear Site Data" or "Clear Cache" settings.

4. GDPR & European Privacy Rights

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom enjoy comprehensive rights:

  • Right of Access & Rectification: Since we do not collect personal profiles, no personal dossiers exist.
  • Right to Erasure & Restriction: You may freely purge local storage on your device.
  • Cookie Consent: We provide a non-intrusive Cookie Consent banner allowing European visitors to accept or customize cookie settings before advertising scripts fire.

5. CCPA & California Privacy Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • We do not sell or share personal information with data brokers.
  • We do not collect sensitive personal data such as social security numbers or biometric information.
  • California residents may exercise opt-out rights for personalized advertising through the designated links above.

6. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA)

Our platform is suitable for users of all ages learning Quran and Salah. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
