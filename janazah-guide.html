صَلَاةُ الْجَنَازَةِ — FUNERAL PRAYER

Complete Step-by-Step Janazah Guide

Salat al-Janazah is a communal obligation (Fard Kifayah) upon the Muslim community. It is offered standing without any bowing (Ruku') or prostration (Sujud), consisting of four Takbirs followed by the Tasleem.

Essential Rules & Standing Posture

  • Fard Kifayah: If a group of Muslims performs it, the obligation is fulfilled for the entire community. If neglected entirely, all who knew of it share the sin.
  • Purity & Facing Qiblah: Perform complete Wudu (or Tayammum if water is unavailable). Both the congregation and the bier (Janazah) face towards the Qiblah.
  • Standing Position of the Imam:
    • For a deceased man: The Imam stands aligned with the head or upper chest.
    • For a deceased woman: The Imam stands aligned with the middle (waist) of the body (as practiced by the Prophet ﷺ, Sunan Abi Dawud #3194).
  • No Adhan or Iqamah: The prayer begins directly with the first Takbir without any call to prayer.
  • No Ruku' or Sujud: The entire prayer is performed while standing upright from start to finish.
TAKBIR 1 OF 4 التَّكْبِيرَةُ الْأُولَىٰ — الثَّنَاءُ وَالْفَاتِحَةُ

Opening Takbir: Sana & Surah Al-Fatihah

The Imam says "Allahu Akbar" loudly and raises hands to the ears/shoulders, and the congregation follows silently. Place the right hand over the left wrist across the chest or below the navel.

Thana with Funeral Addition (سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ):
سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ، وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ، وَتَعَالَىٰ جَدُّكَ، وَجَلَّ ثَنَاؤُكَ، وَلَا إِلَٰهَ غَيْرُكَ.

Subhaanakal-laahumma wa bihamdika, wa tabaarakas-muka, wa ta'aalaa jadduka, wa jalla thanaa'uka, wa laa ilaaha ghayruk.

Meaning: Glory be to You, O Allah, and all praise is Yours. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, magnified is Your praise, and there is no deity worthy of worship besides You.

پاک ہے تیری ذات اے اللہ اور تیرے ہی لیے تمام تعریفیں ہیں، اور با برکت ہے تیرا نام اور بلند و بالا ہے تیری شان، اور عالی ہے تیری ثناء، اور تیرے سوا کوئی معبود نہیں۔

Surah Al-Fatihah (Obligatory in Shafi'i & Hanbali Madhhabs):
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ۝ اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ.

Scholarly Note: In the Shafi'i and Hanbali schools, reciting Al-Fatihah after the first Takbir is an obligatory pillar based on the narration of Ibn Abbas in Sahih al-Bukhari #1335. In the Hanafi school, Thana is standard, while reciting Al-Fatihah is recommended with the intention of supplication (Dua).

TAKBIR 2 OF 4 التَّكْبِيرَةُ الثَّانِيَةُ — الصَّلَاةُ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ ﷺ

Second Takbir: Durood Ibrahim on the Prophet ﷺ

The Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Do not bow or prostrate. Remain standing and recite Durood Ibrahim (the same benediction recited in the Tashahhud of Salah):

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ. اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَىٰ مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ، كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَىٰ آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ.

Allahumma salli 'alaa Muhammadin wa 'alaa aali Muhammad, kamaa sallayta 'alaa Ibraaheema wa 'alaa aali Ibraaheem, innaka Hameedum Majeed. Allahumma baarik 'alaa Muhammadin wa 'alaa aali Muhammad, kamaa baarakta 'alaa Ibraaheema wa 'alaa aali Ibraaheem, innaka Hameedum Majeed.

Meaning: O Allah, send peace upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent peace upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim; indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious. O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and upon the family of Muhammad, as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and upon the family of Ibrahim; indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.

اے اللہ! رحمت نازل فرما حضرت محمد ﷺ پر اور ان کی آل پر جس طرح تو نے رحمت فرمائی حضرت ابراہیم علیہ السلام پر اور ان کی آل پر، بے شک تو قابلِ تعریف اور بڑی بزرگی والا ہے۔ اے اللہ! برکت نازل فرما حضرت محمد ﷺ پر اور ان کی آل پر جس طرح تو نے برکت نازل فرمائی حضرت ابراہیم علیہ السلام پر اور ان کی آل پر، بے شک تو قابلِ تعریف اور بڑی عظمت والا ہے۔

TAKBIR 3 OF 4 التَّكْبِيرَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ — الدُّعَاءُ لِلْمَيِّتِ

Third Takbir: Dedicated Supplications for the Deceased

The Imam says "Allahu Akbar". Recite with sincere devotion the authentic supplication suitable for the deceased.

1. Authentic Dua for Adult Deceased (Male or Female)

Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud #3201, Jami` at-Tirmidhi #1024, Sahih
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِحَيِّنَا وَمَيِّتِنَا، وَشَاهِدِنَا وَغَائِبِنَا، وَصَغِيرِنَا وَكَبِيرِنَا، وَذَكَرِنَا وَأُنْثَانَا. اللَّهُمَّ مَنْ أَحْيَيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَأَحْيِهِ عَلَى الْإِسْلَامِ، وَمَنْ تَوَفَّيْتَهُ مِنَّا فَتَوَفَّهُ عَلَى الْإِيمَانِ. اللَّهُمَّ لَا تَحْرِمْنَا أَجْرَهُ، وَلَا تُضِلَّنَا بَعْدَهُ.

Allahummagh-fir li-hayyinaa wa mayyitinaa, wa shaahidinaa wa ghaa'ibinaa, wa sagheerinaa wa kabeerinaa, wa thakarinaa wa unthaanaa. Allahumma man ahyaytahoo minnaa fa-ahyihee 'alal-Islaam, wa man tawaffaytahoo minnaa fa-tawaffahoo 'alal-Eemaan. Allahumma laa tahrimnaa ajrahoo, wa laa tudillanaa ba'dah.

Meaning: O Allah, forgive our living and our dead, those present among us and those absent, our young and our old, and our males and our females. O Allah, whomsoever You keep alive among us, keep him alive upon Islam, and whomsoever You cause to die among us, cause him to die upon Faith (Iman). O Allah, do not deprive us of his reward, and do not cause us to go astray after him.

اے اللہ! بخش دے ہمارے زندوں کو اور ہمارے مُردوں کو، اور ہمارے حاضرین کو اور ہمارے غائبین کو، اور ہمارے چھوٹوں کو اور ہمارے بڑوں کو، اور ہمارے مَردوں کو اور ہماری عورتوں کو۔ اے اللہ! ہم میں سے جسے تو زندہ رکھے اسے اسلام پر زندہ رکھ، اور جسے ہم میں سے موت دے اسے ایمان پر موت دے۔ اے اللہ! ہمیں اس کے اجر و ثواب سے محروم نہ فرما اور اس کے بعد ہمیں گمراہ نہ کر۔

2. Authentic Dua for a Deceased Minor Child (Pre-Pubescent)

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari (Ta'liqan, Kitab al-Jana'iz), Al-Bayhaqi (As-Sunan al-Kubra 4/9)
اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ لَنَا فَرَطًا، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا أَجْرًا وَذُخْرًا، وَاجْعَلْهُ لَنَا شَافِعًا وَمُشَفَّعًا.

Note: If the deceased child is a girl, pronounce feminine pronouns: اجْعَلْهَا لَنَا فَرَطًا، وَاجْعَلْهَا لَنَا أَجْرًا وَذُخْرًا، وَاجْعَلْهَا لَنَا شَافِعَةً وَمُشَفَّعَةً.

Allahummaj-'alhu lanaa faratan, waj-'alhu lanaa ajran wa thukhran, waj-'alhu lanaa shaafi'an wa mushaffa'aa.

Meaning: O Allah, make him for us an advance herald (predecessor in Paradise), make him for us a source of reward and treasure, and make him for us an intercessor whose intercession is accepted.

اے اللہ! اس بچے کو ہمارے لیے آگے بھیجا ہوا سامان بنا، اور اس کو ہمارے لیے اجر اور ذخیرہ بنا، اور اس کو ہمارے حق میں سفارش کرنے والا اور مقبول شفاعت بنا۔

TAKBIR 4 OF 4 التَّكْبِيرَةُ الرَّابِعَةُ — التَّسْلِيمُ

Fourth Takbir: Final Dua & Salutations of Peace (Tasleem)

The Imam says the 4th "Allahu Akbar". A brief pause for general dua follows, after which the prayer concludes with the Tasleem:

How to Complete the Tasleem:

  • Hanafi School (Two Tasleems): The Imam turns his face to the right saying "As-salāmu 'alaykum wa rahmatullāh", then turns his face to the left repeating "As-salāmu 'alaykum wa rahmatullāh" (based on Sunan Ibn Majah #1518).
  • Shafi'i, Hanbali & Maliki Schools (One Tasleem): The Imam turns his face slightly to the right saying once "As-salāmu 'alaykum wa rahmatullāh" (based on the practice of Abdullah ibn Umar and Abu Hurayrah in Al-Bayhaqi 4/43 and Al-Hakim 1/360).
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ

As-salāmu 'alaykum wa rahmatullāh

May the peace and mercy of Allah be upon you. (آپ پر اللہ کی سلامتی اور اس کی رحمت ہو)

Comparative Juristic (Madhhab) Summary

All four canonical Sunni schools of Islamic jurisprudence agree upon the core structure of Salat al-Janazah (4 Takbirs, no bowing, no prostration). The minor differences are respected variations rooted in authentic Sunnah:

Item / Aspect Hanafi Shafi'i Maliki Hanbali
After 1st Takbir Thana (سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ); Fatihah permitted as dua Surah Al-Fatihah is an obligatory pillar (Rukn) Hamd & praise; Fatihah recommended Surah Al-Fatihah is an obligatory pillar (Rukn)
Raising Hands (Raf' al-Yadayn) First Takbir only In all 4 Takbirs (Sunnah) First Takbir only (well-known view) In all 4 Takbirs (Sunnah)
After 2nd Takbir Durood Ibrahim (Wajib/Sunnah) Durood on the Prophet ﷺ (Rukn) Durood on the Prophet ﷺ (Sunnah) Durood Ibrahim (Rukn)
After 3rd Takbir Supplication for deceased (Sunnah Mu'akkadah) Dua for the deceased is an essential pillar (Rukn) Dua for the deceased is the primary pillar (Rukn) Supplication for deceased is an essential pillar (Rukn)
Tasleem (Salutation) Two Tasleems (right and left) One Tasleem to the right is the pillar; 2nd is Sunnah One Tasleem to the right One Tasleem to the right is the standard Sunnah

Graveyard Etiquette & Condolences (Ta'ziyah)

Visiting graves softens hearts and reminds of the Hereafter. Recite upon entering the cemetery:

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ أَهْلَ الدِّيَارِ مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ، وَإِنَّا إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ بِكُمْ لَاحِقُونَ، نَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ لَنَا وَلَكُمُ الْعَافِيَةَ.

As-salāmu 'alaykum ahlad-diyaari minal-mu'mineena wal-muslimeen, wa innaa in shaa' Allāhu bikum laahiqoon, nas'alul-laaha lanaa wa lakumul-'aafiyah.

Meaning: Peace be upon you, O inhabitants of these dwellings from among the believers and Muslims. And indeed, when Allah wills, we shall join you. We ask Allah for well-being for us and for you. (Sahih Muslim #975)

سلامتی ہو تم پر اے بستی والوں، ایمان والوں اور مسلمانوں میں سے! اور بے شک ہم اگر اللہ نے چاہا تو تم سے ملنے والے ہیں، ہم اللہ سے اپنے لیے اور تمہارے لیے عافیت کے طلبگار ہیں۔

Sunnah Words of Condolence to the Grieving Family:

إِنَّ لِلَّهِ مَا أَخَذَ، وَلَهُ مَا أَعْطَىٰ، وَكُلُّ شَيْءٍ عِنْدَهُ بِأَجَلٍ مُسَمًّى، فَلْتَصْبِرْ وَلْتَحْتَسِبْ.

Inna lillaahi maa akhatha, wa lahu maa a'taa, wa kullu shay'in 'indahoo bi-ajalim-musammaa, fal-tasbir wal-tahtasib.

Meaning: Truly, to Allah belongs what He took, and to Him belongs what He gave, and everything with Him has an appointed time; so be patient and hope for Allah's reward. (Sahih al-Bukhari #1284, Sahih Muslim #923)
